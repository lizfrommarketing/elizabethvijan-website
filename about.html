Her Story

Drive, ambition, and an unwavering determination to help you succeed.

When you choose Elizabeth Vijan as your guide, you're choosing someone who shows up with drive, ambition, and an unwavering determination to help you succeed. She brings a young and hungry mindset to every transaction, which means she negotiates hard, thinks creatively, and advocates for her clients with genuine passion and purpose.

Elizabeth's clients trust her to not only handle the details with accuracy but also to anticipate challenges before they arise, creating a streamlined experience that feels intentional and well-executed at every stage. Most importantly, she treats her clients' goals like her own — communicating quickly, staying on top of every update, and making sure they feel confident and comfortable throughout the process.

Before real estate, Elizabeth worked in property management, where she developed a sharp eye for property condition, tenant relations, and the operational details that make or break a transaction. She holds a business degree in entrepreneurship from the W. P. Carey School of Business at Arizona State University, giving her a founder's mindset when it comes to marketing listings and structuring offers.

Born and raised in Arizona, Elizabeth has had a front-row seat to the Valley's transformation over the past two decades and wants to be part of shaping its future. When she's not helping clients buy or sell, she's usually planning her next trip abroad — she has a genuine passion for travel and learning about other cultures, an outlook she brings into every client relationship: curious, adaptable, and always looking for the best possible outcome.
