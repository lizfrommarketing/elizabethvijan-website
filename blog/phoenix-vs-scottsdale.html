Phoenix vs. Scottsdale: Home Prices, Sales Tax, Airports and Lifestyle Compared for 2026 Buyers

Phoenix or Scottsdale: which is better? Phoenix gives you more house for the money, with an October 2026 single-family median of $481,998, while Scottsdale's was $1,225,000, about two and a half times higher. Scottsdale also has the slightly lower sales tax, at 8.0% combined versus Phoenix's 8.6% retail rate.

Beyond the numbers, the choice usually comes down to lifestyle: Phoenix's range of urban and historic neighborhoods versus Scottsdale's resort-town polish, greenbelt and desert preserve. Here's the side-by-side.

Data note: Market figures are from Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Updates for the City of Phoenix and Scottsdale, October 2026 (current as of November 3, 2026, ARMLS data). Tax rates are from the City of Scottsdale and City of Phoenix. Airport details are from the City of Scottsdale. Read November 19, 2026.

Phoenix vs. Scottsdale at a Glance

Single-family homes, October 2026

  • Median sales price: Phoenix $481,998 (up 2.6%) · Scottsdale $1,225,000 (up 5.6%)
  • Year-to-date median: Phoenix $485,000 · Scottsdale $1,250,000
  • Closed sales: Phoenix 712 · Scottsdale 325
  • Days on market: Phoenix 65 · Scottsdale 75
  • Percent of list price received: Phoenix 97.7% · Scottsdale 96.8%
  • Months of supply: 4.2 in both

Townhouses and condos, October 2026

  • Median sales price: Phoenix $320,000 · Scottsdale $485,000
  • Months of supply: Phoenix 6.7 · Scottsdale 6.6

What it means: both markets are running at a similar pace, so the bigger difference is price. Phoenix homes sold a bit faster and closer to asking in October.

Year-to-date activity

  • Scottsdale: 3,707 single-family closings through October, up 8.0%
  • Phoenix: 8,458, essentially flat (down 0.1%)

Condo buyers have the most room to negotiate in both cities, with more than six months of supply.

Are Phoenix and Scottsdale the Same City?

No. They share a border and a metro area, but Scottsdale has its own city government, police, water utility and tax rates. Scottsdale's population was 243,006 in the Census Bureau's July 2025 estimate, spread across about 184 square miles. More in is Scottsdale part of Phoenix?

Taxes and Everyday Costs

Sales tax

  • Scottsdale: 8.00% combined (5.6% state, 0.7% county, 1.7% city). Voters approved Proposition 490 in 2024, setting the city rate at 1.70% effective July 1, 2025
  • Phoenix: 8.6% combined on its retail chart for rates effective January 1, 2026

Where Scottsdale's sales tax goes

The city says almost 9% of its privilege tax revenue goes to buying land for the McDowell Sonoran Preserve, and almost 18% to transportation.

Other Scottsdale rates worth knowing

  • Use tax: 1.5%
  • Transient lodging tax: 5.0% on stays of 29 days or less, on top of the 1.7% privilege tax on rentals. If you're weighing an investment property, see Scottsdale short-term rental rules

For a full budget picture, see cost of living in Scottsdale.

Airports and Getting Around

  • Phoenix Sky Harbor is the Valley's commercial airport
  • Scottsdale Airport is, in the city's words, a general aviation reliever facility with no commercial commuter or airline service, home to many of the Valley's corporate aircraft

If you'll fly often, check drive times to Sky Harbor from the specific neighborhoods you're considering.

Lifestyle: How They Feel Different

Phoenix

  • Range: downtown high-rises, historic districts, and established neighborhoods like Arcadia at the base of Camelback
  • Urban energy: sports, arts and dining downtown; see is downtown Phoenix a good place to live?
  • Explore: my Central Phoenix guide and Arcadia guide

Scottsdale

  • Old Town: walkable dining, galleries and the canal waterfront
  • Outdoors: the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt and the McDowell Sonoran Preserve
  • Explore: my Scottsdale guide

The in-between options

Neighborhoods near the city line, such as Arcadia and the Camelback corridor, give you a bit of both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Phoenix or Scottsdale better to live in?

Phoenix costs less to buy into; Scottsdale offers Old Town, the greenbelt and the Preserve at a higher price.

What is the difference between Phoenix and Scottsdale?

They're separate cities with different governments, tax rates and price points.

What is the sales tax in Phoenix vs. Scottsdale?

Scottsdale is 8.00% combined; Phoenix's retail rate is 8.6%.

Does Scottsdale have its own airport?

Yes, a general aviation airport with no airline service. Airlines use Sky Harbor.
