Gilbert vs. Scottsdale: Home Prices, Market Pace and Lifestyle Compared for 2026

Gilbert vs. Scottsdale: which is right for you? Gilbert offers a much lower entry price, with an October 2026 single-family median of $611,250, while Scottsdale's median was $1,225,000. Scottsdale trades that premium for Old Town, the McDowell Sonoran Preserve and a larger land area; Gilbert gives you a newer, fast-growing town with more house for the money.

Market tempo: Gilbert homes sold at 98.0% of list price after a median 68 days; Scottsdale homes sold at 96.8% after 75 days, per Phoenix REALTORS.

Data note: Market figures are from Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Updates for Gilbert and Scottsdale, October 2026. Population, housing cost and land-area figures are from U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts. Rates are from Freddie Mac's PMMS (Nov. 12, 2026). Read November 18, 2026.

Home Prices at a Glance

October 2026 single-family numbers:

  • Gilbert median: $611,250, up 5.4% year over year; year to date $599,950, up 1.0%
  • Scottsdale median: $1,225,000, up 5.6%; year to date $1,250,000, up 5.9%

Townhouses and condos are a smaller slice of Gilbert's market: 13 sales in October at a $328,000 median, versus 127 sales at $485,000 in Scottsdale.

What that means monthly

With 20% down at Freddie Mac's 7.11% average:

  • Gilbert median: a $489,000 loan, about $3,290 a month in principal and interest
  • Scottsdale median: a $980,000 loan, about $6,593 a month, and above Maricopa County's 2026 conforming limit, so it's jumbo territory

Taxes, insurance and HOA dues are extra.

Market Pace and Inventory

  • Inventory: Gilbert 916 homes (up 35.7%); Scottsdale 1,480 (up 0.1%)
  • Months of supply: Gilbert 3.9 (up from 2.7); Scottsdale 4.2
  • Pending sales: Gilbert 121 (down 50.2%); Scottsdale 208 (down 43.5%)
  • Days on market: Gilbert 68; Scottsdale 75

The headline: Gilbert has shifted the most. Its supply jumped by more than a full month in a year, so buyers there have noticeably more choice than in 2025.

Town vs. City: The Census Picture

From Census QuickFacts:

  • Population (July 2025): Gilbert 287,285; Scottsdale 243,006
  • Growth since 2020: Gilbert 7.2%; Scottsdale 0.7%
  • Land area: Gilbert 68.57 square miles; Scottsdale 184.00
  • Homeownership rate (2020-2024): Gilbert 73.1%; Scottsdale 67.0%
  • Median owner costs with a mortgage: Gilbert $2,174; Scottsdale $2,577
  • Median gross rent: Gilbert $2,110; Scottsdale $2,013
  • Average commute: Gilbert 25.9 minutes; Scottsdale 21.6

Gilbert packs more residents into far less land, about 3,907 people per square mile versus 1,312 in Scottsdale (2020).

Which One Fits You?

A few questions I walk through with buyers deciding between the two:

  • Budget first: at Gilbert's October median, the estimated principal and interest is roughly half of Scottsdale's, which frees up room for upgrades, a pool or savings
  • Space vs. setting: Gilbert's median buys a detached home in many neighborhoods; in Scottsdale, the same budget often points toward a townhome, condo or an older home to renovate
  • Daily drive: Scottsdale's average commute is shorter, 21.6 minutes versus 25.9, but your own route matters more than any average
  • Pace of the market: Gilbert sellers still get 98.0% of list price on average, so expect less room to negotiate than in Scottsdale, where the figure is 96.8%

Lifestyle: What Each Offers

Gilbert

  • The Riparian Preserve at Water Ranch: a 110-acre wetland with 7 ponds, an urban fishing lake, a floating boardwalk and the Gilbert Rotary Centennial Observatory
  • Newer master-planned neighborhoods and a growing downtown dining scene

Scottsdale

  • The McDowell Sonoran Preserve, more than 30,500 acres with over 230 miles of trails
  • The Indian Bend Wash Greenbelt, 11 miles of parks, lakes and paths
  • Old Town dining, galleries, spring training and resort destinations

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gilbert cheaper than Scottsdale?

Yes. October 2026 medians were $611,250 in Gilbert and $1,225,000 in Scottsdale.

Is Gilbert a city or a town?

A town, with about 287,285 residents in 2025, per the Census Bureau.

Is Gilbert part of Phoenix?

No. It's its own municipality in the East Valley.

Which has more homes for sale, Gilbert or Scottsdale?

Scottsdale had 1,480 single-family listings versus 916 in Gilbert, though Gilbert's inventory is rising faster.
