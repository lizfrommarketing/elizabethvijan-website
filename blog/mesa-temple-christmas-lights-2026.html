Mesa Temple Christmas Lights 2026: Dates, Hours, Parking and Tips for the Valley's Classic Holiday Stroll

When are the Mesa Temple Christmas lights in 2026? Every night from Friday, Nov. 27 through Wednesday, Dec. 30, 5 to 10 p.m., and admission is free. The lights fill the north lawn of the Mesa Arizona Temple at 101 S. LeSueur in downtown Mesa, with free parking nearby and a light rail stop just west of the grounds.

Why it's a Valley tradition: Visit Mesa says the display has run for more than 45 years and draws over a million visitors, with hundreds of thousands of lights on palms, citrus trees, cacti and flower beds. Across the street, the International Nativity Exhibit shows more than 200 nativities from around the world.

Data note: Details are from MesaTemple.org, Visit Mesa's event page (Oct. 15, 2026 release) and the Arizona Communications event toolkit; nearby event details are from the City of Chandler. Read November 13, 2026. Hours can change; confirm before you go.

2026 Details at a Glance

  • Dates: Nov. 27-Dec. 30, 2026 (starts the day after Thanksgiving)
  • Hours: 5-10 p.m. nightly
  • Where: 101 S. LeSueur, Mesa, on Main Street between LeSueur and Hobson
  • Cost: free, no tickets
  • Scenes: 10 lighted scenes telling the Christmas story, each with a QR code for audio narration
  • Accessibility: wide cement and gravel paths suited to strollers and wheelchairs (bring your own)

What You'll See on the Grounds

Visit Mesa describes lights on palm and citrus trees, cacti, hedges and flower beds, plus:

  • A large outdoor creche under a lighted star
  • A shepherd's field
  • Mary and Joseph figurines and the prophet Isaiah
  • Three wise men with camels

MesaTemple.org notes the life-size figurines are the work of Rennie Godfrey of Safford, Arizona, and debuted on the temple grounds in 2005, finished with details such as real sheep pelts.

The International Nativity Exhibit

  • Where: Mesa Temple Visitors' Center, 455 E. Main St., across from the temple
  • Exhibit hours: 5-10 p.m. nightly, plus 11 a.m.-1 p.m. Tuesdays and Thursdays
  • Visitors' Center holiday hours: 10 a.m.-10 p.m. daily
  • What you'll see: 200+ nativities made from materials such as rice, paper, nuts, gourds and porcelain, per Visit Mesa
  • Phone: 480-964-7164

Parking and Getting There

From MesaTemple.org:

  • Free parking may be available at the city's Park and Ride lot, northeast corner of Mesa Drive and Main Street
  • Light rail: Valley Metro has a stop just west of the temple; park-and-ride options include Gilbert and Main and Dobson and Main
  • Special-needs parking: the lot directly east of the north temple grounds on Hobson, with attendants
  • South lot accessible spaces: reserved for temple patrons until after 7:30 p.m. Tuesday-Saturday; open all day Sunday-Monday

From Scottsdale: plan on extra time on weekends, or park at a light rail station and ride in.

Tips for a Better Visit

  • Beat the crowds: organizers suggest arriving right at dusk or after 8 p.m.
  • Weeknights are generally calmer than weekends
  • Bring a jacket: "Desert nights get chilly," as the event toolkit puts it
  • Make it an evening: downtown Mesa's restaurants are steps away on Main Street
  • Photos: the lighted palms and the crèche under the star are the classic shots
