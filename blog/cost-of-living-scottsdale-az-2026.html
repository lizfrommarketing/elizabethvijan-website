Cost of Living in Scottsdale AZ 2026: Housing, Utilities & What to Budget

What is the cost of living in Scottsdale, AZ in 2026? Per Redfin's Cost of Living Calculator (built on C2ER COLI data plus Redfin housing), Scottsdale's overall cost of living is about 15% higher than the national average, with housing about 42% higher. Utilities on that same calculator run about 5% less than the national average — so the premium is real, but it is not evenly distributed across every line item.

If you are comparing "cost of living Scottsdale vs Los Angeles," Denver or Seattle — or asking how Scottsdale stacks against Phoenix — start with housing and then stress-test utilities, groceries and your summer AC bill. One headline percentage is not a budget.

Primary cost-of-living figures below are from Redfin's Cost of Living Calculator for Scottsdale, AZ: https://www.redfin.com/cost-of-living-calculator/scottsdale-az. Housing transaction medians from Phoenix REALTORS® / ShowingTime are a separate data set, labeled clearly.

The Big Picture: Where Scottsdale Sits vs. the U.S.

Redfin Calculator Snapshot (vs. National Average)

  • Overall cost of living: ~15% higher
  • Housing: ~42% higher
  • Groceries: ~6% more
  • Transportation: ~2% more
  • Healthcare: ~2% more
  • Lifestyle: ~6% more
  • Utilities: ~5% less

Housing drives the premium. Everyday categories move less. That is the frame for "average cost of living Scottsdale Arizona."

Housing: Purchase Prices and Rent

Redfin Calculator Housing Illustrations

  • House for sale: Scottsdale $932,500 vs. national average $393,895
  • Apartment rent: Scottsdale $2,028 vs. national $1,621

Treat those as COL illustrations — not a promise for a specific listing tomorrow.

Scottsdale Market Medians (Separate Source)

Phoenix REALTORS® / ShowingTime Local Market Update for Scottsdale, September 2026 (current as of October 3, 2026):

  • Single-family median sales price: $1,200,000 (+4.3% year over year)
  • Single-family average sales price: $1,646,506
  • Months of supply (SFR): 3.7
  • Townhouse/Condo median: $485,000

Source: https://phoenix-public.stats.showingtime.com/docs/lmu/x/Scottsdale

Why both sets? The Redfin calculator answers "how does Scottsdale compare to the U.S. average on a COL index basis?" The REALTORS® update answers "what are recent closed-sale medians in Scottsdale?" They measure different things. Use both — and never mix them into one invented number.

Optional Rent Context (Different Methodology)

Some local market pages (for example Realtor.com) have reported a median rent around $2,409. That is a different methodology (median rent listings) than Redfin's calculator rent of $2,028. Prefer the Redfin figure when you are doing a COL comparison; cite any listing-median rent only with that caveat.

Utilities, Gas and Everyday Costs

Utilities (Redfin Calculator)

Overall utilities ~5% less than national, with sample monthly lines:

  • Energy bill: Scottsdale $251.83 vs. national $227.52
  • Phone: Scottsdale $141.08 vs. national $197.13

Energy here can still run higher than the national energy sample even while the broader utilities category sits below average — phone is the clear offset on that calculator. Summer air conditioning in the Valley matters more than any annual average can show. Budget for peak cooling months, not just a mid-year sample.

Gas

  • Gas: Scottsdale $4.04/gal vs. national $3.24/gal (Redfin calculator)

Sample Grocery Items (Optional Detail from Same Page)

If you like line-item checks, Redfin's page lists sample grocery prices such as bananas at $0.71/lb in Scottsdale vs. $0.77/lb nationally. Individual items swing; the calculator's grocery category overall is about 6% more than national.

Scottsdale vs Phoenix, LA, Denver & Seattle

Scottsdale vs Phoenix

"Scottsdale vs Phoenix cost of living" is really a housing and neighborhood question. Scottsdale's single-family median of $1,200,000 (Sept 2026) sits in a different band than many Phoenix submarkets. Townhouse/condo buyers looking at the $485,000 Scottsdale median may find a closer bridge — still verify by address, HOA fees and condition.

Scottsdale vs Los Angeles, Denver or Seattle

Cross-city searches are fair — and easy to oversimplify. Redfin puts Scottsdale ~15% above the U.S. overall and ~42% above on housing. That does not automatically mean Scottsdale is higher or lower than LA, Denver or Seattle on every line. Run the same calculator for each city, compare housing vs. utilities vs. transportation, and factor commute and taxes separately. State and local taxes differ; this post does not invent tax rankings.

How to Budget Without Surprise

  1. Separate buy vs. rent. Calculator rent and closed-sale medians answer different questions.
  2. Stress-test summer utilities. Peak AC can dominate a Valley electric bill even when a COL category looks mild.
  3. Include HOA and insurance. Neither is fully captured in a simple COL headline.
  4. Compare apples to apples. Same calculator, same date, same housing type when you weigh Scottsdale against another city.
  5. Use a local agent + lender. Medians are not your payment — your rate, down payment and property taxes are.

FAQ: Cost of Living Scottsdale AZ

What is the cost of living in Scottsdale AZ?

About 15% above the national average overall, with housing about 42% higher, per Redfin's Cost of Living Calculator. Utilities are about 5% less than national on that tool.

How does Scottsdale compare to Phoenix?

Scottsdale generally prices higher on single-family homes (citywide SFR median $1,200,000 in Sept 2026). Compare specific areas and property types rather than metro averages alone.

Is Scottsdale cheaper than LA, Denver or Seattle?

It depends on the category. Use the same COL calculator for each city and compare housing, utilities and transportation line by line — do not trust a single viral ranking.

What should I budget for housing?

Redfin's calculator shows illustrative sale and rent figures of $932,500 and $2,028. Recent closed-sale medians from Phoenix REALTORS® / ShowingTime are $1,200,000 (SFR) and $485,000 (townhouse/condo) for Sept 2026. Your payment depends on the property, rate and terms.
