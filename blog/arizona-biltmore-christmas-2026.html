Arizona Biltmore Christmas 2026: Nutcracker Tea, Teddy Bear Tea, Gingerbread Classes and The Bow Bar

What's happening at the Arizona Biltmore for Christmas 2026? The resort's holiday season includes a Nutcracker Tea on November 29 and December 13, a Teddy Bear Tea on December 6 and 20, gingerbread decorating classes on November 28 and December 5, 12 and 19, and The Bow Bar, a Moet & Chandon champagne bar in the Spire Bar space. Letters to Santa begins November 28.

How to book: Call the resort at 602-955-6600 or email Reservations@arizonabiltmore.com. The resort notes limited availability on select dates, so reserve teas and classes early.

Data note: All event details are from the Arizona Biltmore's Holidays page, read November 23, 2026. Neighborhood figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime 85016 Local Market Update for October 2026 (ARMLS data, current as of November 3, 2026). Prices were not listed on the resort's page.

The 2026 Holiday Calendar at a Glance

  • Nov. 28: Letters to Santa begins; first gingerbread decorating class
  • Nov. 29: Nutcracker Tea
  • Dec. 5: Gingerbread decorating class
  • Dec. 6: Teddy Bear Tea
  • Dec. 12: Gingerbread decorating class
  • Dec. 13: Nutcracker Tea
  • Dec. 19: Gingerbread decorating class
  • Dec. 20: Teddy Bear Tea
  • Dec. 31: New Year's Eve at Spire or in the Grand Ballroom

Nutcracker Tea

What it is

An elegant afternoon tea paired with a family-friendly showcase of select performances inspired by The Arizona Nutcracker, presented in partnership with The Madison Center for the Arts.

Dates

  • Sunday, November 29, 2026
  • Sunday, December 13, 2026

If your family loves the ballet, pair it with a full performance. Our guide to The Nutcracker in Phoenix 2026 lists this season's productions.

Teddy Bear Tea

What it is

A festive afternoon tea that the resort describes as combining holiday tradition, the Biltmore's storied setting and a meaningful way to give back.

Dates

  • Sunday, December 6, 2026
  • Sunday, December 20, 2026

Gingerbread Display and Decorating Classes

  • A gingerbread house welcomes guests in the lobby all season
  • Decorating classes: November 28 and December 5, 12 and 19, 2026
  • A good fit for a Saturday with houseguests, and the lobby display is worth a look even if you don't take the class

The Bow Bar

This season the resort's Spire Bar transforms into The Bow Bar, a champagne experience with Moet & Chandon. Expect red bows and shimmering holiday accents, with seating under the desert sky. It's an easy spot for a holiday toast after dinner or a meet-up with friends.

More Holiday Extras

  • Live music around the resort during the season
  • Christmas Dinner To Go for hosting at home
  • Holiday dining specials, seasonal cocktails and spa experiences
  • New Year's Eve: a live DJ and countdown at Spire, or live entertainment, dancing and a midnight toast in the Grand Ballroom

Making It a Holiday Tradition

  • Pick one anchor date. A Sunday tea on Dec. 13 or Dec. 20 leaves the Saturdays before it free for a gingerbread class or the lobby display
  • Bring houseguests on a weeknight. A quiet drink at The Bow Bar is an easy evening when family is in town
  • Give it as a gift. A reserved tea for a neighbor, teacher or new homeowner feels personal and creates a memory
  • Plan for the season's pace. December weekends fill quickly across the Valley, so book first and plan dinner around the reservation

Planning Your Visit

  • Address: 2400 E. Missouri Ave., Phoenix, AZ 85016
  • Reservations: 602-955-6600 or Reservations@arizonabiltmore.com
  • Book early: teas and classes have limited availability
  • Prices: not listed on the resort's page; confirm when you book, along with taxes and gratuity
