Mesa vs. Scottsdale: Home Prices, Monthly Costs and Lifestyle Compared for 2026

Mesa vs. Scottsdale: which is right for you? Mesa offers a far lower entry price, with an October 2026 single-family median of $482,500, while Scottsdale's median was $1,225,000. Scottsdale trades that premium for amenities like the McDowell Sonoran Preserve and Old Town; Mesa gives you more house for the money in a larger, busier city.

Market tempo: both cities are giving buyers room. Mesa homes sold at 98.3% of list price after a median 70 days; Scottsdale homes sold at 96.8% after 75 days, per Phoenix REALTORS.

Data note: Market figures are from Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Updates for Mesa and Scottsdale, October 2026. Housing and geography figures are from U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts. Rates are from Freddie Mac's PMMS, Nov. 12, 2026. Read November 17, 2026.

Mesa vs. Scottsdale at a Glance

Single-family homes, October 2026

  • Median sales price: Mesa $482,500 (down 3.5% year over year); Scottsdale $1,225,000 (up 5.6%)
  • Days on market: Mesa 70; Scottsdale 75
  • Percent of list price received: Mesa 98.3%; Scottsdale 96.8%
  • Homes for sale: Mesa 1,359 (3.6-month supply); Scottsdale 1,480 (4.2 months)
  • Closed sales: Mesa 292; Scottsdale 325

Townhouses and condos, October 2026

  • Median sales price: Mesa $285,000; Scottsdale $485,000
  • Months of supply: Mesa 5.0; Scottsdale 6.6

What It Costs to Own

Census Bureau estimates (2020-2024 American Community Survey):

  • Median value of owner-occupied homes: Mesa $408,000; Scottsdale $789,800
  • Median monthly owner costs with a mortgage: Mesa $1,843; Scottsdale $2,577
  • Median gross rent: Mesa $1,620; Scottsdale $2,013
  • Owner-occupied rate: Mesa 64.4%; Scottsdale 67.0%

At today's rates

With 20% down at Freddie Mac's 7.11% 30-year average, principal and interest alone would run about:

  • Mesa median single-family ($482,500): about $2,597 a month
  • Scottsdale median single-family ($1,225,000): about $6,593 a month

Taxes, insurance and HOA dues are extra. Scottsdale's median also sits above FHFA's $832,750 2026 conforming limit for Maricopa County once you're borrowing 80%, so many buyers there will need jumbo financing; see jumbo loan limits in Arizona.

Size and Shape

  • Population: Mesa about 513,656; Scottsdale about 243,006 (Census, July 2025 estimates)
  • Land area: Mesa 138.7 square miles; Scottsdale 184.0
  • Density: Mesa 3,635.5 people per square mile; Scottsdale 1,311.7 (2020)
  • Average commute: Mesa 24.7 minutes; Scottsdale 21.6

Scottsdale is long and narrow, with large areas of preserve land; Mesa is more compact and more densely built.

Lifestyle Highlights

Mesa

  • Mesa Arts Center at One East Main Street anchors downtown's performance and gallery scene
  • Holiday lights: see our guide to the Mesa Temple Christmas lights
  • Value: a single-family median less than half of Scottsdale's in October 2026

Scottsdale

  • McDowell Sonoran Preserve: more than 30,500 acres and 230-plus miles of trails, per the city
  • Indian Bend Wash Greenbelt: 11 miles of parks, lakes and paths through the middle of town
  • Old Town dining, galleries and events like the WM Phoenix Open at TPC Scottsdale

Reading the Market Trend

The two cities are moving in different directions this fall:

  • Mesa prices eased: the single-family median slipped 3.5% from October 2025, and the year-to-date median through October was $486,250, down 0.8%
  • Scottsdale prices rose: the single-family median was up 5.6% for October, and the year-to-date median through October was $1,250,000, up 5.9%
  • Both cities saw fewer pending sales in October than a year earlier, which gives buyers more time to decide

For sellers, that means pricing to the current month's comparable sales, not last spring's.

How to Decide

  • Budget first: at Mesa's prices, a single-family home costs about what a Scottsdale condo does
  • Daily routine: map your commute, gym and favorite restaurants
  • Resale outlook: both markets have balanced supply right now, so condition and pricing matter more than ever
  • Compare more cities: see Chandler vs. Scottsdale and Tempe vs. Scottsdale

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mesa cheaper than Scottsdale?

Yes. October 2026 single-family medians were $482,500 in Mesa and $1,225,000 in Scottsdale.

Is Mesa a good place to live?

It depends on your priorities: Mesa offers lower prices and a larger city; Scottsdale offers the Preserve and Old Town.

Which city has more homes for sale?

Scottsdale had 1,480 single-family listings in October; Mesa had 1,359.

Is Mesa or Scottsdale better for a condo budget?

Mesa's condo median was $285,000 versus $485,000 in Scottsdale.
