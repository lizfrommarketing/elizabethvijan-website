Is Arcadia a Good Place to Live? Camelback Views, Citrus-Grove Lots and What Homes Cost in Phoenix's 85018

Is Arcadia a good place to live? For buyers who value big lots, mature citrus, Camelback Mountain at the end of the street and a central location between Phoenix and Scottsdale, Arcadia is one of the Valley's most sought-after neighborhoods. The trade-off is price: the 85018 single-family median was $1,725,000 for the first ten months of 2026.

Where it is: the area commonly called Arcadia runs north of the Arizona Canal and south of Camelback Road, between 44th Street and Scottsdale Road. Most of it is in Phoenix; the part east of 64th Street is in Scottsdale.

Arcadia at a Glance

  • Boundaries (common usage): Arizona Canal to Camelback Road, 44th Street to Scottsdale Road
  • City: mostly Phoenix; east of 64th Street is Scottsdale
  • ZIP code: much of it is in 85018
  • Signature features: large lots, citrus, a historic irrigation system, Camelback Mountain views
  • Nearby resorts: the Royal Palms and the Phoenician, both on Camelback Road

Why Arcadia Looks the Way It Does

Arcadia's character comes from how it was planned more than a century ago:

  • 1919: the original Arcadia plat divided land east of 56th Street into lots of about ten acres and west of 56th Street mostly into lots of about five acres, sold as small citrus orchards
  • The water: developers formed the Arcadia Water Company in 1919, and by 1924 crews had laid 15 miles of underground concrete irrigation pipe
  • A surviving landmark: the building that is now the Shemer Art Center was built in 1919 for the water company's foreman
  • Mid-century growth: by the mid-1950s, Phoenix's growth reached Arcadia, and many of today's ranch homes date from the late 1950s and early 1960s

That history explains the wide lots, tall citrus and green lawns you still see. The City of Phoenix plan also credits the Arizona Canal to the south and Camelback Mountain to the north with keeping the area quiet and insulated from through traffic.

Living Here: The Day-to-Day

Camelback Mountain

  • Echo Canyon Trailhead: 4925 E. McDonald Drive, open sunrise to sunset, with restrooms, potable water and a small shade canopy
  • Echo Canyon Trail: 1.14 miles one way and about 1,400 feet of elevation change, rated Extremely Difficult by the city
  • Summit: 2,704 feet above sea level
  • Know before you go: the lot typically fills from late morning on winter and spring weekdays and all day on weekends, and dogs are prohibited on all Camelback trails

Dining and the canal

Camelback Road and Indian School Road anchor much of the neighborhood's dining, and the Arizona Canal path runs along Arcadia's southern edge.

Location

Arcadia sits between central Phoenix and Old Town Scottsdale. The City of Phoenix plan lists relatively easy access to downtown Phoenix among the reasons the area stays in demand.

What Homes Cost: 85018 in October 2026

Single-family

  • Year-to-date median: $1,725,000, up 16.9%, on 387 closings
  • October median: $2,113,500, up 30.5%, a month skewed by luxury sales
  • Days on market: 65 in October
  • Percent of list price received: 95.9%
  • Inventory: 199 homes, 5.4 months of supply

Townhouses and condos

  • October median: $345,000
  • Months of supply: 7.7

The read: single-family supply sits in balanced territory, and sellers gave up about 4% from list on average in October. That's real room to negotiate on a seven-figure home. For the full breakdown, see the Arcadia real estate market 2026.

The Honest Trade-Offs

  • Price of entry: single-family homes are mostly a seven-figure purchase
  • Older homes: many mid-century houses have been renovated, and those that haven't may need system updates. Budget for inspections
  • Irrigation questions: if a home has irrigation service, ask how it's delivered, on what schedule and who maintains it
  • Trailhead traffic: streets near Echo Canyon see heavy visitor parking demand in peak season
  • Arcadia vs. Arcadia Lite: the name is used loosely in listings. See Arcadia vs. Arcadia Lite and my Arcadia and Arcadia Lite pages
