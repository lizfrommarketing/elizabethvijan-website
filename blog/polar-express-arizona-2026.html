The Polar Express in Arizona 2026: Dates, Ticket Prices, Departure Times and Tips for the Grand Canyon Railway Ride from Williams

Where is the Polar Express in Arizona? Grand Canyon Railway runs The Polar Express from its depot in Williams on select evenings from Nov. 6, 2026 through Jan. 3, 2027, with departures at 5:30 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. The round trip takes about 90 minutes and includes hot chocolate, cookies, a reading of the story and a visit from Santa.

How much it costs: train-only tickets run in three tiers by date, from $64.99 for adults and $49.99 for children on Value nights up to $84.99 and $69.99 on Holiday nights, before taxes and fees. Holiday-season weekends tend to sell first.

Data note: Departure times, location, refund policy, package details and accessibility notes are from Grand Canyon Railway's official Polar Express and booking pages. Season dates are from AZFamily (Oct. 26, 2026). Ticket tiers are from Raising Arizona Kids (Nov. 11, 2026, sponsored by Grand Canyon Railway). Read November 25, 2026. Confirm prices on the booking calendar.

The Essentials

  • Where: Grand Canyon Railway Depot, Williams, directly behind the Grand Canyon Railway Hotel (233 N. Grand Canyon Blvd.)
  • When: select evenings, Nov. 6, 2026 to Jan. 3, 2027
  • Departures: 5:30 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. Arizona time
  • Length: about 90 minutes round trip
  • On board: hot chocolate and chocolate chip cookies, the story read aloud, and Santa
  • Booking: online or 1-888-848-3511

The railway notes the sun sets before 5:30 p.m. for the whole season, so both departures ride in the dark with the lights on.

2026 Ticket Prices

Per Raising Arizona Kids' 2026 guide (prices per person, before taxes and fees):

  • Value: $64.99 adults (16+), $49.99 children (2 to 15)
  • Standard: $74.99 adults, $59.99 children
  • Holiday Limited: $84.99 adults, $69.99 children

Lower prices are generally on earlier-season and weekday departures. The railway's booking calendar shows each date's tier.

The Package Option

Grand Canyon Railway's Polar Express Package bundles:

  • One night in a hotel room or suite, saving 10%
  • One dinner and one breakfast per person at the Fred Harvey Restaurant, each saving 10%
  • Round-trip Polar Express tickets

Package rates are based on adult double occupancy and exclude tax. Staying over makes sense for a 7:30 p.m. departure, when the drive back to the Valley would run well past bedtime.

Booking Rules Worth Reading

From the railway's reservation policies:

  • Full payment is due at booking
  • Refunds: for 2026 reservations booked on or after Aug. 10, 2026, a full refund up to 14 days before departure, 50% within 14 days
  • Changes: allowed up to 7 days before travel, limited to adding guests or switching dates within the season
  • Pickup: will-call at the Williams depot at least 30 minutes before departure, with photo ID matching the reservation name
  • Non-transferable tickets
  • Groups: more than 9 train-only tickets go through Central Reservations
  • Accessibility: not all rail cars are ADA accessible; call 1-800-843-8724 at least 7 days ahead to arrange

Tips From a Valley Local

  • Pick your departure: the 5:30 p.m. train gets little ones back earlier; the 7:30 p.m. train pairs well with an overnight stay
  • Leave buffer: you have to pick up tickets 30 minutes early, and northern Arizona winter weather can slow I-17
  • Dress warm: Williams is in the high country, and December nights there are a world away from Scottsdale
  • Book soon: popular December dates, especially weekends and the days before Christmas, fill quickly, per Raising Arizona Kids
