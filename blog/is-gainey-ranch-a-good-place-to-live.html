Is Gainey Ranch a Good Place to Live? Gates, Golf, the Estate Club and What Homes Cost in Scottsdale's 85258

Is Gainey Ranch a good place to live? It's a gated master-planned community in central Scottsdale with walls and electronic gates around every residential area, three security plazas, officers on patrol 24 hours a day, a 10,000-square-foot Estate Club, and a private 27-hole golf club at its center. It offers a lock-and-go, resort-style setup, paid for through two layers of HOA dues.

What it costs to buy in: Phoenix REALTORS® doesn't publish Gainey Ranch-only data, but in ZIP 85258 the October 2026 single-family median was $1,235,000 and the townhouse/condo median was $632,500.

Data note: Community facts are from the Gainey Ranch Community Association (GRCA) website. Golf facts are from Gainey Ranch Golf Club (Invited) and Experience Scottsdale. Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS® Local Market Update for ZIP 85258, October 2026, which covers more than Gainey Ranch. Read November 25, 2026.

Where Is Gainey Ranch?

Gainey Ranch sits in central Scottsdale, ZIP 85258, between Old Town and the northern part of the city. Two landmarks anchor it:

  • Gainey Ranch Golf Club: 7600 E. Gainey Club Drive
  • Grand Hyatt Scottsdale Resort: 7500 E. Doubletree Ranch Road

It shares a ZIP code with McCormick Ranch, which is why the two are often compared. See is McCormick Ranch a good place to live for that side of the map.

How the HOA Works: Two Layers

The GRCA explains its structure clearly:

  • Every owner of Gainey Ranch property, residential or not, is a GRCA member
  • Eighteen residential communities each have their own satellite association
  • Owners belong to both the GRCA and their satellite, and pay both monthly assessments
  • The GRCA counts 1,035 residential memberships, one vote per lot or unit
  • A five-person board governs; four seats are elected by residential members
  • Satellites must use the GRCA for landscape and building maintenance, which keeps upkeep uniform

For buyers: request both budgets and reserve studies during your inspection period. The architectural committee reviews changes, with a $45 submittal fee for major projects, and all tree and landscaping removals or installs require approval.

Security: The Signature Amenity

The GRCA says surveys show security is residents' most important amenity. What's in place:

  • Walls, fences and electronic gates around all residential areas
  • Three main security plazas, plus unmanned electronic gates with cameras
  • Officers patrolling 24 hours a day, CPR certified and carrying first-aid equipment
  • An alarm system in every residence, with basic monitoring included in dues

The Estate Club

The community's clubhouse is a 10,000-square-foot building with:

  • A pool and a fitness center (no charge for homeowners)
  • Tennis and pickleball (a separate tennis membership fee applies)
  • A wine room with a fireplace, a bar, card rooms and a library
  • Fitness classes, personal training and massage
  • A shaded patio with heaters and fans, and a BBQ area

Golf: Private, 27 Holes

Gainey Ranch Golf Club offers three nine-hole courses that combine into 18-hole rounds:

  • The Lakes: 3,376 yards, par 36
  • The Dunes: 3,253 yards, par 36
  • The Arroyo: 3,424 yards, par 36

Michael Poellot and Bradford L. Benz designed them. The club is private; Experience Scottsdale lists play as limited to members and guests of the resort. The club sells its own memberships, including full golf and limited golf (May 1 through Oct. 31), separate from your HOA dues. Some homes sit right on the fairways; a current Arroyo Vista listing on the GRCA site, for example, describes a home at the Arroyo course's 7th tee.

Homes: Detached and Condos

The GRCA lists single-family communities such as Arroyo Vista, The Enclave, The Estates, Golf Villas, The Greens, Lakeview, The Legend, North Meadow and Vaquero Drive, and condominium communities including The Courts, The Golf Cottages, The Pavilions, The Oasis and Sunset Cove.

ZIP 85258, October 2026 (Phoenix REALTORS®):

  • Single-family median: $1,235,000, down 8.1% year over year
  • Single-family days on market: 76
  • Single-family months of supply: 3.1
  • Townhouse/condo median: $632,500, up 16.4%
  • Townhouse/condo days on market: 91

Monthly ZIP medians move with the mix of homes that close, so a single month is a snapshot, not a trend. For the citywide picture, Scottsdale's single-family median was $1,225,000 in October.

Everyday Logistics

  • Trash: the GRCA notes City of Scottsdale pickup on Tuesdays, recycling on Thursdays
  • Old Town: a short drive south for dining and galleries
  • Resort next door: the Grand Hyatt Scottsdale, on Doubletree Ranch Road
