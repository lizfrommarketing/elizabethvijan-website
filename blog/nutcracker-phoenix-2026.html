The Nutcracker in Phoenix 2026: Ballet Arizona Dates, Showtimes, Tickets, Parking and Tips

When is The Nutcracker in Phoenix? Ballet Arizona performs Ib Andersen's The Nutcracker December 11 through 27, 2026, at Symphony Hall in downtown Phoenix, with Tchaikovsky's score played live by The Phoenix Symphony. Ballet Arizona lists 18 performances, from opening night on Friday, Dec. 11 at 7:30 p.m. to the Sunday, Dec. 27 matinee at 1 p.m.

Plan ahead: Ballet Arizona's subscription page notes the production sells out each year, and the company had already flagged the Saturday, Dec. 12 matinee as "Low Tickets!" when we checked.

Data note: Dates, showtimes, venue, runtime, content guidance, parking and policies are from Ballet Arizona's official Nutcracker page and its season subscription page. Read November 19, 2026. Showtimes can change, so confirm when you buy.

The Nutcracker 2026 at a Glance

  • Dates: December 11 to 27, 2026
  • Venue: Symphony Hall, 75 N. 2nd St., Phoenix, AZ 85004
  • Music: live, by The Phoenix Symphony, conducted by Matthew Kasper
  • Choreography: Ib Andersen
  • Runtime: about two hours, including one intermission
  • Best for: all ages, per Ballet Arizona
  • Box office: 602-381-1096

Sensory-adapted performance (Dec. 16)

An abbreviated Act II performance with softened recorded music, softer lighting, designated quiet spaces, fidget devices and freedom to move in and out of the theater.

What You'll See

Ballet Arizona's synopsis follows Clara from her family's Christmas party, where the mysterious Dr. Drosselmeyer gives her a wooden nutcracker, through a midnight battle with the Mouse King and a sleigh ride to the Land of the Sugar Plum Fairy. Act II brings the familiar dances: Spanish Chocolate, Arabian Coffee, Chinese Tea, the Russian Trepak, plus the Dew Drop Fairy and her Waltzing Flowers.

  • Sets: Carey Wong and Ib Andersen
  • Costumes: Fabio Toblini
  • Content note: brief theatrical fog, atmospheric lighting and a battle scene with toy cannons; no strobe effects
  • With little ones: booster seats are available from coat check on the west side of the lobby

Angel Night

Each season, Ballet Arizona welcomes hundreds of children, families and community members to a performance free of charge. The company says a $50 gift welcomes one child to their first ballet.

Tickets and the VIP Upgrade

Buy through Ballet Arizona's website or box office so you know you're paying face value.

Capture the Magic VIP package

  • Price: $125 with parking, with a reduced rate the week of the performance
  • Includes: lobby entry 90 minutes early, a photo with the Sugar Plum Fairy, four hot chocolate vouchers, a parking pass, autographed pointe shoes, a tote, a $20 gift card and 20% off at the Nutcracker Boutique
  • Timing tip: the garage accepts only physical parking passes, so buy at least a week ahead to have yours mailed

Changing plans

Contact the box office before your performance. Exchanges carry a $5 per ticket fee, and box office credit expires June 12, 2027.

Getting There: Parking, Light Rail and Security

  • Best garage: Phoenix Convention Center West Garage on 2nd Street, next door
  • Other garages: Regency, Civic Plaza West, Chase, Heritage, Convention Center East and Wells Fargo
  • Light rail: stops at Washington/Central Ave., Jefferson/3rd St. and Jefferson/1st Ave.
  • Bags: clutches up to 8.5 x 5.5 inches and clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches; medical and family bags allowed; everyone passes through metal detectors
  • Doors: lobby, will call and box office open one hour before showtime
  • No late seating, so leave extra time

Make It a Holiday Night Out

  • Before the show: downtown Phoenix restaurants within a short walk of Symphony Hall
  • Another night: Christmas at the Princess in Scottsdale or Phoenix ZooLights
  • Skating: our ice skating guide
