Things to Do in Scottsdale in November 2026: Canal Convergence, Polo, Art Festivals and Holiday Lights

What's happening in Scottsdale in November 2026? The month opens with Canal Convergence (Nov. 6-15), free light-based art along the Scottsdale Waterfront, then a big first weekend with the Bentley Scottsdale Polo Championships (Nov. 7) and the Kierland Fine Art & Wine Festival (Nov. 7-8). It closes with holiday tree lightings on Nov. 28 in Old Town and at Scottsdale Quarter.

Is November a good time to be out and about? It's one of the busiest. Scottsdale Living notes November highs typically land in the 70s, which is why outdoor festivals stack up. Book dinners early and plan for parking on the big weekends.

Data note: Dates, hours and prices come from Experience Scottsdale, Old Town Scottsdale, Scottsdale Quarter, Scottsdale Living and event organizers, reviewed Oct. 23, 2026. Events can change; confirm with each organizer before you go.

Canal Convergence: Western Nature (Nov. 6-15)

  • Where: Scottsdale Waterfront, along the canal
  • Cost: Free
  • Theme: Western Nature, honoring Arizona and the Southwest
  • What to expect: large-scale light-based public art, nightly fire shows, live music and dance, workshops, rotating food trucks and a beer and wine garden

Hours (per Experience Scottsdale):

  • Fri.-Sat., Nov. 6-7: 6-10 p.m.
  • Sun.-Thu., Nov. 8-12: 6-9 p.m.
  • Fri.-Sat., Nov. 13-14: 6-10 p.m.
  • Sun., Nov. 15 (closing night): 6-9 p.m.

The Big First Weekend (Nov. 7-8)

Bentley Scottsdale Polo Championships (Sat., Nov. 7)

  • 15th anniversary of the event
  • WestWorld Art & Wine Experience with 100 nationally juried artists
  • England Select vs. France Select, plus the Molina Cup
  • Arabian horse Western Pleasure competition at halftime
  • Previews of Barrett-Jackson and Phoenix Fashion Week, and a sunset concert

Kierland Fine Art & Wine Festival (Nov. 7-8)

  • Free admission; art 10 a.m.-5 p.m.
  • 100+ artists and 15 Arizona wineries
  • 21+ tasting tickets sold separately

Also That Weekend

  • Italian Festival of Arizona: Nov. 7-8
  • Carefree Garden Art Experience: Nov. 7-8, free, 10 a.m.-5 p.m.
  • Arizona Fall Fest: Nov. 7
  • Día de Muertos at The Garden: Nov. 6-9
  • Water Lantern Festival: Nov. 6-8 at Kiwanis Lake in Tempe; tickets $30.99-$57.99

Mid-Month: Art, Pizza and Country Music (Nov. 13-15)

  • Fountain Festival of Fine Arts & Crafts: Nov. 13-15
  • Herberger Theater Festival of the Arts: Sat., Nov. 14
  • Phoenix Pizza Festival: Nov. 14-15
  • Hondo Rodeo Fest at Chase Field: Nov. 13-15
  • Nov. 13: Tim McGraw and Miranda Lambert
  • Nov. 14: Blake Shelton and Turnpike Troubadours
  • Nov. 15: Parker McCollum and Lynyrd Skynyrd
  • Free Hondo Street Fest daily from noon

Thanksgiving Weekend: The Holidays Light Up (Nov. 28)

Scottsdazzle Tree Lighting Spectacular

  • Saturday, Nov. 28, at the Scottsdale Waterfront
  • Theme: Celebrating 75 Years of Scottsdale
  • Live music, seasonal food vendors and the lighting of a 36-foot holiday tree
  • Scottsdazzle continues through Dec. 31 with 20+ signature events, including Merry Main Street, Shop & Strolls, Santa at the Farmers Market and Pancakes & Pajamas with Santa
  • Free public parking in Old Town garages and surface lots

Scottsdale Quarter Tree Lighting

  • Saturday, Nov. 28, 5:30-7:30 p.m., at The Quad
  • Tree lights at 7 p.m.
  • Live music, ballet, toy soldiers, Santa photos for purchase and a hot chocolate bar
  • $5 raffle tickets benefit Phoenix Children's

Also Nov. 28

  • Phoenix Flea: its 13th annual Small Business Saturday market

Want lights closer to Chaparral Park? McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park's Holiday Lights open in late November; see our sister site's holiday lights guide.

FAQ: Scottsdale in November

What events are in Scottsdale in November 2026?

Canal Convergence (Nov. 6-15), Scottsdale Polo (Nov. 7), Kierland art and wine (Nov. 7-8) and tree lightings (Nov. 28).

Is Canal Convergence free?

Yes. It runs 6-10 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays and 6-9 p.m. other nights.

Is November a good time to visit Scottsdale?

Highs typically land in the 70s, and it's one of the busiest event months.

When does Scottsdazzle start in 2026?

Nov. 28, with the Tree Lighting Spectacular, running through Dec. 31.
