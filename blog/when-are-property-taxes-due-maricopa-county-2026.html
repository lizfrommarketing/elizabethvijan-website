When Are Property Taxes Due in Maricopa County? 2026 Deadlines, Delays & Penalties

When are property taxes due in Maricopa County? Arizona splits the bill in two. The first half is due October 1 and the second half is due March 1. What matters most is the delinquency date: for 2026, the Maricopa County Treasurer lists first-half taxes as delinquent after 5 p.m. on Monday, November 2, 2026, because November 1 lands on a Sunday.

Is there a way to skip the March payment? Yes. If you pay the full year by December 31, Arizona law waives interest on the first half even if you missed November. And if your bill hasn't arrived, don't wait: the Treasurer's Office says 2026 bills were mailed late this year, and you can look yours up and pay online now.

Data note: Due dates and interest rules come from Arizona Revised Statutes 42-18052 and 42-18053 and the Maricopa County Treasurer's property tax calendar (checked Oct. 22, 2026). The mailing delay was reported by AZFamily on Sept. 30, 2026.

The 2026 Maricopa County Property Tax Calendar

  • Oct. 1, 2026: first half of 2026 taxes due
  • Nov. 2, 2026, 5 p.m.: first half becomes delinquent and interest starts
  • Dec. 31, 2026: last day to pay the full year without interest (bills over $100)
  • Mar. 1, 2027: second half due
  • May 1, 2027: second-half delinquency date. It's a Saturday, so the statute's weekend rule moves it to 5 p.m. on the next business day

What the Law Actually Says

  • Two halves: one-half due October 1, the rest due the following March 1 (A.R.S. 42-18052)
  • Delinquency: after 5 p.m. November 1 and 5 p.m. May 1
  • Weekends and holidays: the deadline moves to 5 p.m. the next business day
  • Small bills: $100 or less is due in full October 1 and delinquent after December 31
  • The lien: a property tax lien attaches on January 1 of the tax year (A.R.S. 42-17153)

Why Some 2026 Bills Showed Up Late

If your bill arrived after October 1, you're not imagining it. AZFamily reported on September 30 that the Treasurer's Office attributed the delay to late changes in the property tax levy and rates and technical problems during the rollout of a new computer system. The office said taxpayers won't be penalized if they pay the first half by 5 p.m. Monday, November 2.

You don't need the paper bill to pay. Search by parcel number or address on the Maricopa County Treasurer's website and pay online.

What Happens If You Pay Late

  • Interest: 16% per year, simple, from the moment of delinquency (A.R.S. 42-18053)
  • Partial months: count as whole months
  • The December 31 exception: no interest if the full year is paid on or before December 31 of the tax year
  • Long-term delinquency: unpaid taxes can be offered at the county's tax lien sale, where the winning bidder pays the delinquent amount and the owner later redeems the lien with interest

How Your Bill Is Calculated, in Brief

Your tax is driven by your Limited Property Value (LPV), not your home's market value. Under A.R.S. 42-13301, LPV is last year's LPV plus 5%, and it can never exceed the assessor's full cash value. That's why a big jump in home prices doesn't translate one-for-one into your tax bill. Rates set by your city, school district and other taxing districts do the rest.

Do You Pay It, or Does Your Lender?

  • Escrowed loan: if your monthly mortgage payment includes taxes, your servicer pays the Treasurer. Check your annual escrow statement
  • No escrow or paid off: the bill is yours to pay. Mark November 2 and March 1
  • Buying or selling: taxes are prorated at closing. See what it costs to sell a house in Arizona

FAQ: Maricopa County Property Taxes

When are property taxes due in Arizona?

First half October 1 (delinquent after 5 p.m. November 1); second half March 1 (delinquent after 5 p.m. May 1).

When is the 2026 first-half deadline in Maricopa County?

5 p.m. Monday, November 2, 2026, because November 1 is a Sunday.

What is the penalty for paying Arizona property taxes late?

16% per year simple interest; partial months count as full months. Pay the full year by December 31 and no interest applies.

Does Arizona have a senior property tax freeze?

Maricopa County's Senior Valuation Protection freezes the LPV, not the tax, for three years. The deadline is September 1, and 2027 applications open after January 1, 2027.
