Glendale vs. Scottsdale: Home Prices, Rents, Sales Tax and Lifestyle Compared (October 2026 Data)

Glendale or Scottsdale? Glendale is far more affordable, with a $420,000 single-family median in October 2026 versus $1,225,000 in Scottsdale, while Scottsdale offers a resort-and-preserve lifestyle at roughly three times the price. Glendale homes also sold faster and closer to asking, and its sales tax is higher.

Bottom line: if budget and West Valley access lead your list, Glendale stretches your dollar. If Old Town, golf, trails and the East Valley's resort scene matter most, Scottsdale is the trade-up.

Data note: Home-sale figures are from Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Updates for October 2026 (ARMLS data, current as of November 3, 2026). Rents are from the Zillow Observed Rent Index (ZORI), October 2026. Sales tax rates are from the City of Glendale's combined tax rate chart and the City of Scottsdale's Taxes page. Read November 20, 2026.

Home Prices, October 2026

Single-family homes

  • Glendale median: $420,000 (flat year over year); year to date $440,000
  • Scottsdale median: $1,225,000 (up 5.6%); year to date $1,250,000

Townhouses and condos

  • Glendale median: $235,000; year to date $230,000
  • Scottsdale median: $485,000; year to date $495,000

That's nearly a $805,000 gap at the single-family median, which is why many buyers compare the two only after deciding what lifestyle they're paying for.

How Fast Homes Are Selling

  • Days on market: 60 in Glendale vs. 75 in Scottsdale (single-family)
  • Percent of list price received: 98.9% vs. 96.8%
  • Inventory: 744 Glendale homes (up 21.8%) vs. 1,480 in Scottsdale (flat)
  • Months of supply: 4.1 vs. 4.2
  • Condo supply: 6.0 months in Glendale vs. 6.6 in Scottsdale

Translation: Glendale's lower price points still move quickly, while Scottsdale buyers typically have a bit more room to negotiate.

Market Size and Price Range

  • Average single-family sale: about $463,000 in Glendale vs. about $1,618,000 in Scottsdale (October)
  • Closed single-family sales, year to date: 1,950 in Glendale vs. 3,707 in Scottsdale
  • New single-family listings in October: 246 in Glendale vs. 523 in Scottsdale
  • Pending sales in October: 87 in Glendale (down 53.2%) vs. 208 in Scottsdale (down 43.5%)

Pending sales dropped sharply in both cities from a year earlier, a sign buyers pulled back as rates climbed. Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed average reached 7.28% on November 19, 2026. If you're shopping now, that slower pace is your leverage, whichever city you choose.

Rents

  • Glendale: about $1,499 a month, up about 1.6% from a year earlier
  • Scottsdale: about $2,142, up about 3.1%

ZORI reflects typical asking rents across all rental types.

Sales Tax

  • Glendale combined: 9.2% for most retail (5.6% state + 0.7% county + 2.9% city); 10.2% at restaurants and bars
  • Scottsdale combined: 8.00% for most business activities (5.6% state + 0.7% county + 1.7% city)

On $10,000 of furniture and décor for a new home, that's roughly $920 in Glendale versus $800 in Scottsdale. (Glendale applies a 2.2% city rate to single items of $5,000 or more.)

Lifestyle: How They Feel Different

Glendale

  • Westgate Entertainment District surrounding State Farm Stadium, home of the Arizona Cardinals, and Desert Diamond Arena
  • Historic Downtown Glendale, home to the holiday tradition Glendale Glitters
  • Spring training at Camelback Ranch
  • West Valley location for commutes toward the Loop 101 and Loop 303 corridors

Scottsdale

  • Old Town dining, galleries and nightlife
  • McDowell Sonoran Preserve trails across the north end of the city
  • Golf and resorts, including TPC Scottsdale, home of the WM Phoenix Open
  • Spring training at Scottsdale Stadium

For more on Scottsdale's price points, see Phoenix vs. Scottsdale and our cost of living in Scottsdale guide.

Which One Fits Your Plans?

Glendale may fit if you want:

  • More square footage or a larger lot for the budget
  • A lower entry price for a first home or an investment property
  • Quick access to West Valley employers and the stadium district

Scottsdale may fit if you want:

  • Walkable dining and arts in Old Town
  • Trail access to the McDowell Sonoran Preserve
  • Golf, resort amenities and a broad luxury market

Many buyers start with a budget, then let a weekend in each city settle it. Drive your actual commute at rush hour, too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes: $420,000 vs. $1,225,000 single-family medians in October 2026.

Glendale or Scottsdale: which has higher sales tax?
