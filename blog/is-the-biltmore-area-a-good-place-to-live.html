Is the Biltmore Area a Good Place to Live? Phoenix 85016 Homes, Prices, Shopping and the Camelback Corridor

Is the Biltmore area a good place to live? For many Phoenix buyers, yes: it offers a central Camelback Road location, the historic Arizona Biltmore resort, Biltmore Fashion Park's 60-plus shops and restaurants, and a wide range of homes, from condos to custom estates. It sits in ZIP 85016, a short drive from Arcadia, downtown Phoenix and Sky Harbor.

What it costs right now: Phoenix REALTORS reports an October 2026 single-family median of $805,000 in 85016, up 10.1% from a year earlier. The townhouse and condo median was $501,389, which is why many buyers start here with a condo.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Update for ZIP 85016, October 2026. 85016 also covers neighborhoods beyond the Biltmore area, so treat it as a proxy. Rents are Zillow ZORI, October 2026. Resort history is from the Arizona Biltmore; shopping details are from Biltmore Fashion Park. Read November 24, 2026.

The Biltmore Area at a Glance

  • ZIP code: 85016 (Arizona Biltmore and Biltmore Fashion Park both use it)
  • Anchor: the Arizona Biltmore, opened Feb. 23, 1929
  • Shopping: Biltmore Fashion Park at 2502 E. Camelback Rd., with 60-plus shops and restaurants and a Life Time club
  • Setting: the Camelback Corridor, with office towers, restaurants and resort views of Camelback Mountain
  • Home types: high-rise and mid-rise condos, townhomes, golf-course homes and custom single-family estates

A Little History

The Arizona Biltmore traces its story to the McArthur brothers, who wanted to make Arizona a destination. Albert Chase McArthur designed the resort with a strong Frank Lloyd Wright influence, drawing on Wright's block design. After the 1929 stock market crash, William Wrigley Jr. bought the property. The resort's history page also credits its bar with inventing the Tequila Sunrise in the 1930s.

85016 Market Snapshot, October 2026

Single-family homes

  • Median: $805,000, up 10.1% (year to date: $765,000, flat)
  • Closed sales: 20, up from 16
  • Days on market: 73, down from 118
  • Share of list price received: 95.3%
  • Inventory: 112 homes, 4.7 months of supply

Townhouses and condos

  • Median: $501,389, up 16.1% (year to date: $410,000, down 1.2%)
  • Inventory: 159, 7.5 months of supply
  • Share of list price received: 97.2%

Year to date (single-family)

  • Closed sales: 253, up 11.9% from the same period in 2025
  • Pending sales: 243, up 7.5%
  • New listings: 369, down 5.1%

More homes have closed in 85016 this year than last, even as new listings dipped.

Rents: Zillow's ZORI shows typical 85016 rent near $1,645 in October 2026, up 2.3% from a year ago.

Reading the numbers: single-month medians in one ZIP swing with the mix of homes that closed. The year-to-date figures are steadier, and with 7.5 months of condo supply, condo buyers have plenty to choose from.

Daily Life Around the Biltmore

  • Shopping and dining: Biltmore Fashion Park describes itself as an outdoor center with gardens and fountains, and its Life Time club includes a rooftop beach club with views of Camelback Mountain
  • The resort: the Arizona Biltmore's seasonal events, like its holiday teas and gingerbread classes, take reservations from the public
  • Getting around: Camelback Road and 24th Street connect you to Arcadia, Central Phoenix and the freeways

Biltmore vs. Nearby Areas

  • Arcadia (85018): larger lots and ranch homes east of the Biltmore area. See Arcadia vs. Arcadia Lite
  • Scottsdale: the October 2026 single-family median was $1,225,000, per Phoenix REALTORS
  • Biltmore's edge: walkable shopping and condo options in a central Phoenix location

What to Check Before You Buy

  1. HOA dues and reserves, especially in high-rise and gated buildings
  2. Rental and short-term rental rules, which vary by community
  3. Parking, storage and guest access in condo buildings
  4. Traffic and noise along Camelback Road and 24th Street at different times of day
