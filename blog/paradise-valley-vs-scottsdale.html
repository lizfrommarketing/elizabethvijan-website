Paradise Valley vs. Scottsdale: Taxes, Lot Sizes & 2026 Home Prices Compared

Is Paradise Valley part of Scottsdale? No. Paradise Valley is its own town, incorporated on May 24, 1961, with about 12,774 residents (2025 estimate) on 15.4 square miles between Phoenix and Scottsdale. It has its own Town Council, police department and zoning code. Mail is delivered from Scottsdale's Hopi Station post office, which is one reason the two names blur together.

Is Paradise Valley or Scottsdale more expensive? Paradise Valley, by a wide margin. In September 2026, the single-family median sales price was $4,185,000 in Paradise Valley versus $1,200,000 in Scottsdale, per Phoenix REALTORS®. The real differences sit underneath those numbers: lot sizes, taxes and how each place is built.

Data note: Town facts are from the Town of Paradise Valley's Basic Town Facts page and Resident's Guide. Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS® Local Market Updates for September 2026 (single-family, ARMLS data current as of October 3, 2026). Figures don't account for seller concessions.

Paradise Valley at a Glance

  • Incorporated: May 24, 1961
  • Population: 12,658 (2020 Census); 12,774 (2025 estimate)
  • Households: 5,046 (2020 Census)
  • Land area: 15.4 square miles
  • Within its borders: 9 resorts, 14 places of worship, 11 public and private schools, 3 golf courses and 4 medical centers
  • ZIP code: 85253

The Three Differences That Matter Most

1. Lot Size and Zoning

Paradise Valley is predominantly zoned for single-family homes, one home per lot. Most of the Town is zoned R-43, meaning each lot must be at least one acre. Other districts include:

  • R-175: 4-acre lots
  • R-35: 35,000 square feet
  • R-18: 18,000 square feet
  • R-10: 10,000 square feet

Some R-43 lots are smaller than an acre because they were annexed that way; the Town calls them legal non-conforming lots. Scottsdale, by contrast, spans everything from Old Town condos to north-side estates, so "Scottsdale" can mean a lock-and-leave townhome or a gated golf property.

2. Property Taxes

The Town's Resident's Guide states plainly that the Town does not assess a primary or secondary property tax. That doesn't mean a zero tax bill:

  • Still collected: Maricopa County, State of Arizona, school district and special district levies
  • How the Town is funded: revenue sources include sales and bed taxes from its resorts

3. Services

  • Police: the Town's own Paradise Valley Police Department
  • Fire: through a partnership with the City of Phoenix Fire Department
  • Water: one of three providers depending on the address: City of Phoenix, EPCOR or Berneil Water Company

That last point is a due-diligence item: in Paradise Valley, confirm the water provider for the specific home you're considering.

FAQ: Paradise Valley vs. Scottsdale

Is Paradise Valley part of Scottsdale?

No. It's a separate town, incorporated in 1961, with its own council, police and zoning.

Is Paradise Valley or Scottsdale more expensive?

Paradise Valley: a $4,185,000 September 2026 median versus $1,200,000 in Scottsdale (single-family).

Does Paradise Valley have property tax?

No Town property tax, but county, state, school and special-district taxes still apply.

Why is Paradise Valley AZ so expensive?

Mostly one-acre minimum lots (R-43) in a town of 15.4 square miles.
