Does Arizona Have a Senior Property Tax Freeze? How Maricopa County's Senior Valuation Protection Works

Does Arizona have a senior property tax freeze? Yes, in a sense. Arizona's Senior Valuation Protection program, often called the "senior freeze," lets eligible homeowners freeze the Limited Property Value (LPV) of their primary residence for three years. In Maricopa County, at least one owner must be 65 or older, and household income must fall under $47,712 for one owner or $59,640 for two or more owners.

The key fine print: it freezes the value used to calculate taxes, not the tax bill. Tax rates can still change, and the Assessor notes your bill may fluctuate.

Data note: Program rules, income limits and deadlines come from the Maricopa County Assessor's Senior Valuation Protection page and 2026 application. Valuation rules come from A.R.S. 42-13301 and 42-13302. Other Arizona counties administer the program through their own assessors.

How the Senior Freeze Works

Limited Property Value vs. Full Cash Value

Arizona property has two values on your notice:

  • Full Cash Value (FCV): the Assessor's estimate of market value
  • Limited Property Value (LPV): the value your property taxes are calculated on

Under A.R.S. 42-13301, LPV generally rises no more than 5% per year, and it cannot exceed full cash value. The senior freeze goes further: it holds LPV at the current tax year's value for three consecutive years.

What the Freeze Does Not Do

  • It doesn't freeze the tax rate. Rates set by taxing jurisdictions can still change
  • It doesn't eliminate taxes at any age
  • It doesn't transfer to a new home

Who Qualifies in Maricopa County

Per the Assessor's 2026 rules, you must meet all of these:

  • Age: at least one owner is 65 or older
  • Residency: the home has been your primary residence for at least two years
  • Income: income from all sources for all owners on title does not exceed:
  • $47,712 for one owner
  • $59,640 for two or more owners
  • Averaging: income is averaged over the last three years
  • What counts: Social Security and veterans disability payments are included, and business losses must still be reported
  • Property type: rental property does not qualify

Documents You'll Need

  • A completed, signed application
  • Proof of two years' residency (driver license, state ID or voter card)
  • Proof one owner is 65+
  • Income documentation: the first two pages of federal Form 1040 for 2023, 2024 and 2025 for all owners, or SSA-1099 and other 1099s if you didn't file

Deadlines and Renewals

  • Application window: January 1 through September 1 of the tax year
  • Late applications roll to the following tax year
  • 2027 applications open after January 1, 2027
  • Renewal is required every three years
  • Opting out is allowed anytime, but you must wait one year to reapply
  • You can combine it with a personal exemption if you qualify for both

Contact: Maricopa County Assessor, 602-506-3406, or apply online through the Assessor's website.

What Happens if You Sell, Move or Remodel

Senate Bill 1224, enacted September 26, 2025, changed what happens when protection ends. The Assessor lists these triggers:

  • A change in ownership
  • The home is no longer your primary residence
  • Certain property changes you request
  • The parcel is split or merged
  • A value change of more than 15% from construction or destruction
  • Not renewing

When eligibility ends, LPV is recalculated under A.R.S. 42-13302 using a "Rule B" calculation, which sets it at a level similar to comparable properties without the protection. In plain terms, the buyer of a frozen home doesn't inherit the freeze.

Planning Tips

  • Thinking about a big remodel or an addition? Ask the Assessor how it could affect eligibility first
  • Estate planning? A beneficiary deed can transfer a home at death; the new owner's eligibility is separate
  • Selling? Escrow prorates taxes; see when Maricopa County property taxes are due

FAQ: Arizona Senior Property Tax Freeze

Does Arizona have a senior property tax freeze?

Yes. Senior Valuation Protection freezes LPV for three years, not the tax rate.

Who qualifies for the senior freeze in Arizona?

An owner 65+, two years of primary residence, and income under $47,712 / $59,640.

When can I apply for Senior Valuation Protection in Maricopa County?

January 1 through September 1. Renew every three years.

At what age do seniors stop paying property taxes in Arizona?

None. 65 is the minimum age for the freeze, which lowers growth in taxable value, not the tax itself.
