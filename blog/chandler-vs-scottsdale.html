Chandler vs. Scottsdale: Home Prices, Size, Commutes and Lifestyle Compared for 2026

Chandler or Scottsdale? Chandler generally offers more house for the money and a faster-moving market; Scottsdale offers a wider spread of luxury, resort and desert-preserve living at a much higher price point. In October 2026, Phoenix REALTORS reported a median single-family sale price of $535,000 in Chandler and $1,225,000 in Scottsdale.

The cities are close in population but not in shape. The Census Bureau estimates 278,748 Chandler residents and 243,006 in Scottsdale (July 2025), but Scottsdale stretches across 184 square miles to Chandler's 65. That difference in land shapes everything from lot sizes to how far you drive for dinner.

Data note: Market figures are from Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Updates for Chandler and Scottsdale, October 2026. Population, housing and commute figures are from U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts (V2025 estimates and 2020-2024 ACS). Event details are from the City of Chandler. Read November 16, 2026.

Chandler vs. Scottsdale at a Glance

Home prices (October 2026, Phoenix REALTORS)

  • Single-family median: Chandler $535,000 (down 7.4% year over year); Scottsdale $1,225,000 (up 5.6%)
  • Townhouse/condo median: Chandler $337,500; Scottsdale $485,000
  • Percent of list price received (single-family): Chandler 98.1%; Scottsdale 96.8%

Market pace (single-family)

  • Days on market: Chandler 56; Scottsdale 75
  • Months of supply: Chandler 3.3; Scottsdale 4.2
  • Homes for sale: Chandler 626; Scottsdale 1,480

Census snapshot

  • Median owner-occupied home value (2020-2024): Chandler $507,800; Scottsdale $789,800
  • Median monthly owner costs with a mortgage: Chandler $2,044; Scottsdale $2,577
  • Median gross rent: Chandler $1,902; Scottsdale $2,013
  • Homeownership rate: Chandler 65.0%; Scottsdale 67.0%
  • Mean commute: Chandler 23.9 minutes; Scottsdale 21.6

Size and Shape: Why It Matters

  • Density: Chandler had about 4,226 people per square mile in 2020, versus about 1,312 in Scottsdale
  • Scottsdale's range: from south Scottsdale's mid-century neighborhoods to Old Town, McCormick Ranch and the far north near the McDowell Mountains
  • Chandler's footprint: a more compact suburban city anchored by its historic downtown

If you love the idea of Scottsdale but want a lower entry point, the townhouse and condo market ($485,000 median) or south Scottsdale may bridge the gap. For more side-by-sides, see Tempe vs. Scottsdale and Fountain Hills vs. Scottsdale.

Lifestyle and Holiday Traditions

Chandler

  • Downtown Chandler centers on Dr. A.J. Chandler Park, with restaurants and shops around the square
  • Tumbleweed Tree Lighting & Parade of Lights: Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, free; the city says the roughly 34-foot tree debuted in 1957, making 2026 its 70th year

Scottsdale

  • Old Town for dining, galleries and nightlife
  • Retail draw: Scottsdale's retail sales per capita were $47,864 in 2022, about double Chandler's $22,757, per the Census Bureau
  • Big-ticket winter events, including the WM Phoenix Open at TPC Scottsdale

How to Decide Between Them

  • Budget first: at October medians, the single-family gap is about $690,000, which changes your loan size, down payment and property taxes
  • Map your weekday: test the drive to work, the gym and the airport at rush hour, not on a Sunday morning
  • Compare home age and lot size: ask for year built and lot dimensions on every listing; both cities include homes from many eras
  • Check the address-level costs: HOA dues, electric provider and water and trash rates all vary by property
  • Look neighborhood by neighborhood: citywide months of supply can hide very different conditions a few miles apart

Who Wins on Value Right Now?

  • Chandler: prices eased in October and homes still sold at 98.1% of list, a sign of steady demand at mid-market prices
  • Scottsdale: prices rose year over year, but more inventory and longer market times give buyers negotiating room
  • Financing: with the 30-year fixed at 7.11% on Nov. 12 (Freddie Mac), the price gap matters even more for your payment

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Chandler cheaper than Scottsdale?

Yes. October 2026 single-family medians were $535,000 in Chandler and $1,225,000 in Scottsdale.

Is Chandler or Scottsdale bigger?

Chandler has more residents (278,748 vs. 243,006); Scottsdale has nearly three times the land.

Do homes sell faster in Chandler or Scottsdale?

Chandler: 56 days and 3.3 months of supply in October. Scottsdale: 75 days and 4.2 months.

How far is Chandler from Scottsdale?

Both are in the East Valley, linked by Loop 101 and Loop 202. Drive times vary widely by location and time of day.
