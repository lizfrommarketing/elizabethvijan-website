How Long Does It Take to Close on a House in Arizona? Your 2026 Escrow Timeline

How long does it take to close on a house in Arizona? With a mortgage, plan on about five to six weeks from accepted offer to keys. ICE's May 2026 Mortgage Monitor found the average U.S. purchase loan closed in 36.8 days in March 2026, the fastest since ICE began tracking in 2019: about 11 days from application to rate lock, then 26 more to closing.

What sets the pace in Arizona? The contract does. In the standard Arizona resale contract, your closing date must be a specific date, and the big deadline is the 10-day inspection period, counted in calendar days, weekends included. Close of escrow happens when the deed is recorded at the County Recorder's Office. Here's how the weeks typically unfold.

Data note: Closing-time data are national figures from ICE's May 2026 Mortgage Monitor (March 2026 loans). Contract terms are from the Phoenix REALTORS® sample Residential Resale Purchase Contract (December 2024) and the Arizona REALTORS® sample resale contract (November 2025). Your contract's actual terms control.

The Arizona Escrow Timeline, Week by Week

Day 0: Offer Accepted

  • Acceptance occurs when the signed contract, and any counteroffer, is delivered to and received by the appropriate broker
  • Earnest money goes to the escrow company
  • Counting rule: "days" are calendar days, starting the day after acceptance and ending at 11:59 p.m.

Example (arithmetic, not advice): an offer accepted Wednesday, October 21, 2026 starts Day 1 on Thursday, October 22, and a 10-day inspection period runs through 11:59 p.m. Saturday, October 31.

Days 1–10: Inspection Period

  • Default length: 10 days for residential resales in both sample contracts
  • What happens: inspections, insurance quotes, HOA documents, survey or well checks if they matter to you
  • Seller's disclosure (SPDS): under the Arizona REALTORS® form, due within 3 days after acceptance
  • Insurance claims history: within 5 days (Arizona REALTORS® form)
  • HOA information: under 50 units, the seller provides it within 10 days; 50+ units, the seller gives escrow the HOA contact within 5 days

Repair Requests

  • Buyer: one signed notice of objections before the inspection period ends
  • Seller: 5 days to respond in writing; silence counts as a refusal
  • Buyer: then 5 days to cancel or proceed with what the seller agreed to fix
  • Receipts: agreed repairs are due 3 days before close of escrow

Weeks 2–4: Appraisal and Loan Approval

  • Appraisal contingency: if the home appraises below price, the buyer has 5 days after notice of the appraised value to cancel and recover earnest money
  • Underwriting: income, assets and the property itself are verified

Final Week: Walk-Through and Closing

  • Walk-through: confirms the home is in substantially the same condition as at acceptance
  • Signing: buyer and seller sign escrow documents
  • Close of escrow: when the deed records with the County Recorder

What Can Slow an Arizona Closing

  • Appraisal gaps that reopen negotiations
  • HOA document delays, especially in larger associations
  • Repair disputes that run past the 5-day windows
  • Loan conditions that arrive late in underwriting
  • Title issues, such as unreleased liens

Missed deadlines have teeth. The contract calls time "of the essence," and a party that doesn't perform can receive a cure notice with 3 days to fix the problem.

Can You Close Faster?

Often, yes. Without a lender, the loan steps drop out, and your timeline is driven by title, your inspection period and the date you negotiate. With a loan, a locked rate and documents ready on day one make the biggest difference.

FAQ: Closing in Arizona

How long does it take to close on a house in Arizona?

About 5–6 weeks with a loan; the average purchase loan closed in 36.8 days (ICE, March 2026).

Does the 10-day inspection period include weekends in Arizona?

Yes. Contract days are calendar days, starting the day after acceptance.

Is Arizona an escrow state?

Resale closings run through an escrow company; closing occurs when the deed records.

What happens to earnest money in Arizona if the deal falls apart?

Returned if you cancel within the inspection period or appraisal contingency; disputes go to mediation.
