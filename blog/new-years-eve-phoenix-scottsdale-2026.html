Phoenix and Scottsdale New Year's Eve 2026: Countdowns, Fireworks, Concerts and Tickets for Dec. 31

What's happening for New Year's Eve 2026 in Phoenix and Scottsdale? The biggest new option is NYE Party on the Park at Scottsdale Civic Center, with The Docksiders and a drone show right after midnight. W Scottsdale is hosting Midnight Mirage with fireworks, The Phoenix Symphony rings in 2027 with Arturo Sandoval, and Decadence Arizona takes over Phoenix Raceway. New Year's Eve falls on a Thursday this year.

Plan around ticketed events. These celebrations are ticketed, and early-bird pricing for Party on the Park ends November 30.

Data note: Event details are from each organizer's official page: Scottsdale Arts (event page and Oct. 6, 2026 release), Cottontail Lounge at W Scottsdale, The Phoenix Symphony and Decadence Arizona. Fireworks rules are from A.R.S. 36-1606 and the City of Phoenix Fire Department. Read November 24, 2026. Confirm times and prices before you buy.

Quick Picks for Dec. 31

  • Old Town outdoor party: NYE Party on the Park, Scottsdale Civic Center, from 8 p.m., 21+
  • Fireworks with a view: Midnight Mirage at W Scottsdale
  • Concert and champagne: The Phoenix Symphony with Arturo Sandoval, 7:30 p.m.
  • Big music festival: Decadence Arizona, Phoenix Raceway, Dec. 30 and 31, 18+

NYE Party on the Park (Scottsdale Civic Center)

Scottsdale Arts, Pinyon and AZ88 are launching this inaugural celebration in the heart of Old Town.

  • When: Thursday, Dec. 31, 8 p.m.
  • Entertainment: local radio host Rich Berra, DJ Slick 76 and The Docksiders, the yacht rock band fresh from a Las Vegas residency
  • Midnight: a countdown followed immediately by a drone show
  • Included: red-carpet arrival and two drink tickets; cash bars and food for purchase
  • Tickets: $150 + $11 fee through Nov. 30; $175 + fee Dec. 1 to 30; $200 + fee on Dec. 31
  • VIP lounge: $4,000 for up to eight, with bottle service and valet
  • Good to know: 21+; clear bags only; re-entry only with a reservation at AZ88 or Pinyon

Midnight Mirage at W Scottsdale

Cottontail Lounge at W Scottsdale (7277 E. Camelback Rd.) hosts Midnight Mirage, with fireworks, a midnight toast and VIP tables.

  • Stay package: includes a room, two party tickets, a $40 beverage credit and festive accessories; you must stay on Dec. 31
  • Reservations: 602-405-0099

The Phoenix Symphony: New Year's Eve Celebration

  • When: Thursday, Dec. 31, 7:30 p.m.
  • Who: Principal Pops Conductor Stuart Chafetz with 10-time Grammy winner Arturo Sandoval and his band
  • Extra: a complimentary champagne toast on arrival
  • Box office: 602-495-1999

Decadence Arizona (Phoenix Raceway)

  • When: Dec. 30 and 31, 2026
  • Where: Phoenix Raceway, 7602 Jimmie Johnson Dr., Avondale
  • Hours: doors 6 p.m., ends 3 a.m. each night
  • Age: strictly 18+
  • For the countdown: buy the Dec. 31 ticket

Fireworks at Home: Arizona's Rules

Under A.R.S. 36-1606, cities in Maricopa County can prohibit consumer fireworks outside set dates. The City of Phoenix lists these:

  • Use dates: Dec. 26 through Jan. 4
  • Late-night window: 11 p.m. Dec. 31 to 1 a.m. Jan. 1; otherwise cities can ban use from 11 p.m. to 8 a.m.
  • Allowed in Phoenix: sparklers, smoke devices, ground spinners and fountains
  • Prohibited: rockets, bottle rockets, aerial shells and anything that rises into the air and explodes
  • Preserves: cities can ban use within one mile of large city-owned preserves at all times

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do on New Year's Eve 2026 in Phoenix and Scottsdale?

Party on the Park, Midnight Mirage, The Phoenix Symphony and Decadence Arizona.

Are there New Year's Eve fireworks in Scottsdale?

Yes, at W Scottsdale's Midnight Mirage, plus a drone show at Scottsdale Civic Center.

Can I set off fireworks on New Year's Eve in Arizona?

Permissible ground fireworks only, Dec. 26 to Jan. 4, and 11 p.m. to 1 a.m. on New Year's.

How much are tickets for NYE Party on the Park?

From $150 plus fees through Nov. 30.
