Phoenix Symphony Holiday Pops 2026: Dates, Showtimes, Parking, Plus Elf in Concert, Messiah and Holiday Baroque

When is the Phoenix Symphony Holiday Pops in 2026? The Holiday Pops Spectacular plays Symphony Hall in downtown Phoenix December 4-6, 2026, with four performances: Friday at 7:30 p.m., Saturday at 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., and Sunday at 2 p.m. Guest conductor Daniel Wiley leads the orchestra and the Phoenix Symphony Chorus in carols and hymns from various traditions.

Planning more than one night out? The Symphony's holiday season starts Thanksgiving weekend with Elf in Concert (Nov. 27-29), continues with Handel's Messiah, including two Scottsdale performances, and wraps with Holiday Baroque at the Orpheum Theatre (Dec. 18-20).

Data note: Dates, times, venues, performers and parking details are from The Phoenix Symphony's Holiday Pops Spectacular page, season calendar and Parking and Venues page. Read November 20, 2026. Confirm times and prices when you buy.

Holiday Pops Spectacular at a Glance

  • Dates: Friday, Dec. 4 through Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026
  • Showtimes: Fri. 7:30 p.m. · Sat. 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. · Sun. 2 p.m.
  • Venue: Symphony Hall, 75 N. Second Street, Phoenix
  • Length: about 2 hours 15 minutes
  • Series: PHOENIX POPS®, sponsored by APS

Who's on stage

  • Daniel Wiley, guest conductor
  • Katherine Henly, soprano
  • Reginald Smith, Jr., baritone
  • The Phoenix Symphony Chorus, with Chorus Master Dr. Thomas Bookhout

The Symphony describes the program as festive carols and hymns from various traditions. A detailed program list hadn't been posted when we checked.

Getting There From Scottsdale

Parking

  • Reserve ahead through ACE Parking online or in its app for Symphony Hall performances
  • Garages in that system: Phoenix Convention Center Regency Garage and West Garage
  • Collier and 305 garages: available through ACE's search up to 30 days ahead
  • The Symphony notes it doesn't control parking prices and garages fill up quickly, so arrive early

Light rail

  • Closest stops: 3rd St. & Washington (westbound) and 3rd St. & Jefferson (eastbound)
  • Park & Ride: the Symphony lists 38th & Washington and Central Avenue & Camelback Road

Box office: (602) 495-1999, Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-5 p.m.

More Phoenix Symphony Holiday Concerts

Elf in Concert, Thanksgiving weekend

  • When: Friday-Sunday, Nov. 27-29, 2 p.m. each day
  • Where: Symphony Hall
  • Why go: an easy post-Thanksgiving outing with out-of-town guests

Handel's Messiah, Dec. 9-13

  • Wed., Dec. 9, 7:30 p.m.: St. Thomas Aquinas Catholic Church
  • Thu., Dec. 10, 7:30 p.m.: Camelback Bible Church
  • Fri., Dec. 11, 7:30 p.m.: Mesa Arts Center
  • Sat., Dec. 12 and Sun., Dec. 13, 3 p.m.: Pinnacle Presbyterian Church, 25150 N. Pima Road, Scottsdale

The Pinnacle Presbyterian matinees are the closest Symphony holiday performances to north Scottsdale.

Holiday Baroque, Dec. 18-20

  • Fri., Dec. 18 and Sat., Dec. 19, 7:30 p.m.; Sun., Dec. 20, 2 p.m.
  • Where: Orpheum Theatre, 203 W. Adams Street, Phoenix

The Nutcracker

The Phoenix Symphony also plays live for Ballet Arizona's Nutcracker at Symphony Hall in December. See our Nutcracker in Phoenix 2026 guide.

Venue Quick Reference

  • Symphony Hall: 75 N. Second Street, Phoenix; owned and operated by the City of Phoenix
  • Orpheum Theatre: 203 W. Adams Street, Phoenix; owned and operated by the City of Phoenix
  • Mesa Arts Center, Ikeda Theater: 1 E. Main Street, Mesa
  • Pinnacle Presbyterian Church: 25150 N. Pima Road, Scottsdale

Box office for all four: (602) 495-1999 or (800) 776-9080. Mesa Arts Center also lists its own box office at (480) 644-6500.

Which Show Fits Your Plans?

  • Want a big, festive sing-along mood? Holiday Pops, with a full orchestra, chorus and soloists
  • Hosting family over Thanksgiving weekend? Elf in Concert, with all three shows as 2 p.m. matinees
  • Prefer a sacred-music tradition close to home? Messiah at Pinnacle Presbyterian in Scottsdale
  • Looking for something intimate? Holiday Baroque at the historic Orpheum

Make a Night of It

  • Pair it with lights: Phoenix Zoo ZooLights and Las Noches de las Luminarias are holiday staples
  • Matinee strategy: the Saturday 2 p.m. Pops show leaves time for dinner downtown or back in Scottsdale
  • Gift idea: tickets make an easy experience gift for parents or new neighbors
