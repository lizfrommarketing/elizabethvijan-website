First-Time Home Buyer Programs in Arizona 2026: Down Payment Assistance Explained

What first-time home buyer programs are available in Arizona in 2026? Two of the most searched options are Arizona is Home (Home in Five Advantage) and Home Plus down payment assistance. Both can help eligible buyers with down payment and/or closing costs through a forgivable second loan, paired with approved first mortgages — but income limits, education, county coverage and forgiveness timelines differ.

If you searched "first time home buyer arizona down payment," "arizona home plus" or "az is home down payment assistance," this guide summarizes published program facts only. A participating lender — not a blog post — confirms whether you qualify.

Program details below are taken from the program sites linked in each section. Rules change. Always verify with the program and your lender before you write an offer.

Arizona is Home (Home in Five Advantage)

Program site: https://homein5advantage.com/azishome
Also see: Arizona Department of Housing — https://housing.az.gov/general-public/arizona-home-program

Down Payment Assistance

  • DPA options: 3%, 4%, 5% or 6%
  • Structured as a 7-year forgivable second loan
  • Interest-free second; no payments unless the home is sold, transferred or refinanced before the 84th month

First Mortgage Products

  • 30-year fixed FHA, VA, Fannie Mae HFA Preferred or Freddie Mac HFA Advantage

Who Qualifies (Published Rules)

  • First-time homebuyer: has not owned residential property in the last 3 years
  • Arizona resident for a minimum of 6 months
  • Minimum FICO 640
  • Maximum credit-qualifying income cannot exceed $131,520 for all borrowers (as stated on the program page)
  • 1% origination fee plus customary closing costs at closing
  • Occupy as principal residence within 60 days
  • Complete 8-hour homebuyer education through an approved Arizona counseling agency
  • Home must be in Maricopa County
  • New purchases only
  • Property types: houses, condos, PUDs, townhouses
  • No purchase price limit (per program page)

The Arizona Department of Housing page notes the Arizona Home Program covers Maricopa and Pima counties and references 120% AMI or below. This draft does not invent AMI dollar amounts — confirm current AMI tables with Housing.az.gov or your lender.

Home Plus Down Payment Assistance

Program site: https://homeplusaz.com/home-plus-dpa/

Key Terms

  • Available in all Arizona counties (statewide)
  • Up to 4% DPA for down payment and/or closing costs
  • Borrower annual income not to exceed $155,386.00 (as of 4/6/2026)
  • One borrower must complete homebuyer education before closing
  • Forgivable second after 5 years / 60 months
  • If you sell or refinance within 60 months, you repay the DPA
  • Per their site: available year-round and does not deplete funding
  • Rates set by the Arizona IDA and may be slightly higher than market — compare total cost with your lender

Home Plus is often the better geographic fit when the purchase is outside Maricopa County, or when income sits above Arizona is Home's published cap but within Home Plus limits — your lender maps which path fits your file.

Quick Comparison

  • Geography: Arizona is Home — Maricopa County (ADH also notes Maricopa & Pima); Home Plus — all Arizona counties
  • DPA: Arizona is Home 3%–6% options; Home Plus up to 4%
  • Forgiveness: 7 years (84 months) vs. 5 years (60 months)
  • Published income caps: $131,520 (Arizona is Home, all borrowers) vs. $155,386.00 (Home Plus, as of 4/6/2026)
  • Education: 8-hour approved counseling (Arizona is Home) vs. one borrower before closing (Home Plus)

How to Move Forward Without Getting Ahead of Yourself

  1. Talk to a participating lender first. They confirm income, credit, property eligibility and which program fits.
  2. Complete education early. Both paths require homebuyer education before closing (Arizona is Home: 8-hour approved agency).
  3. Budget the full stack. DPA helps; you may still owe an origination fee (Arizona is Home: 1%) and customary closing costs.
  4. Compare rate + assistance. Home Plus notes rates may run slightly higher than market — model the payment, not just the down payment help.
  5. Write offers with your agent once financing is clear. An agent helps with strategy; the lender confirms eligibility.

FAQ: Arizona First-Time Buyer Assistance

What first-time home buyer programs exist in Arizona?

Arizona is Home / Home in Five Advantage and Home Plus DPA are two primary options with published down payment assistance. Confirm current terms on their sites and with a participating lender.

How much down payment assistance can I get?

Arizona is Home lists 3%, 4%, 5% or 6% DPA options. Home Plus offers up to 4%. Exact availability depends on the loan and your approval.

What are the income limits?

Arizona is Home: max credit-qualifying income $131,520 for all borrowers (program page). Home Plus: borrower annual income not to exceed $155,386.00 as of 4/6/2026. ADH also references 120% AMI — verify current AMI dollars with the agency or lender.

Do these work for Scottsdale purchases?

Possibly, if you meet program rules and a participating lender approves the property and loan. Scottsdale SFR medians are high; many buyers explore townhouse/condo inventory or other financing. Eligibility is rules-based, not neighborhood-based.
