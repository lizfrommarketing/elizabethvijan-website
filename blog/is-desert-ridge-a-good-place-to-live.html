Is Desert Ridge a Good Place to Live? Home Prices, Rents and What Mayo Clinic's $2.2 Billion Expansion Means for North Phoenix

Is Desert Ridge a good place to live? For many buyers, yes: Desert Ridge offers newer master-planned neighborhoods, Desert Ridge Marketplace, resort golf and quick Loop 101 access, at a single-family median of $660,000 in October 2026, roughly half of Scottsdale's citywide median. It's in the City of Phoenix, mostly in ZIP 85050.

What's new: On Mayo Boulevard near 56th Street, close to Desert Ridge, Mayo Clinic's Phoenix campus expansion is now a $2.2 billion project, according to the Phoenix Business Journal on November 20, 2026, up from the nearly $1.9 billion Mayo announced in March 2025.

Data note: Home sales figures are from Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Updates for ZIPs 85050 and 85054 and the City of Scottsdale, October 2026 (ARMLS data, current as of November 3, 2026). Rents are Zillow Observed Rent Index, October 2026. Mayo Clinic details are from the Mayo Clinic News Network (March 2025), Fierce Healthcare and the Phoenix Business Journal (November 20, 2026). Read November 23, 2026.

Desert Ridge at a Glance

  • City: Phoenix, northeast side, bordering Scottsdale
  • Main ZIP: 85050, with neighboring areas in 85054
  • Getting around: Loop 101 runs along the area, with SR-51 to the west
  • Everyday amenities: Desert Ridge Marketplace for shopping, dining and entertainment
  • Resort and golf: JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge Resort & Spa and its Wildfire golf courses
  • Nearby: Kierland and the Scottsdale Airpark to the east; see is Kierland in Phoenix or Scottsdale?

What Homes Cost: 85050, October 2026

Single-family

  • Median sale price: $660,000, up 17.9% from $560,000
  • Year-to-date median: $710,000, up 8.7%
  • Closed sales: 34, up from 25
  • Days on market: 56; sellers received 97.4% of list price
  • Inventory: 101 homes, 3.3 months of supply

Townhouse/condo

  • Median: $397,500 on just two October closings; the year-to-date median was $420,000

Next door in 85054

  • Single-family median: $980,000 in October on seven sales; $1,080,000 year to date

For comparison

  • Scottsdale citywide single-family median: $1,225,000 in October 2026
  • Phoenix citywide: $481,998

What It Costs to Rent

  • 85050: about $2,473 a month in October 2026, up about 4.3% from a year earlier (Zillow ZORI)
  • 85054: about $1,950, roughly flat year over year
  • Scottsdale citywide: about $2,142

The Mayo Clinic Expansion, in Plain Numbers

  • Budget: now reported at $2.2 billion (Phoenix Business Journal, Nov. 20, 2026), up from nearly $1.9 billion announced in March 2025
  • Size: about 1.2 million square feet of expansion, increasing clinical space on the Phoenix campus by nearly 60%
  • What's included: a new procedural building, an expanded Specialty Building, 11 new operating rooms and two patient units with 48 additional beds
  • Jobs: more than 3,500 new jobs expected for the region, per Mayo Clinic in Arizona's CEO
  • Timeline: completion slated for 2031, with some parts opening earlier

What it may mean for housing

  • Steady demand nearby. Thousands of new jobs within a short drive tend to support both purchase and rental demand
  • Patience required. The hiring phases in over several years, not all at once
  • Traffic and construction. Expect work along Mayo Boulevard for years. Visit at rush hour before you buy

Is Desert Ridge Right for You? Start With What You Want Nearby

Make a short list of what matters day to day, then compare:

  • Shopping and dining within a short drive, without heading to Old Town
  • Golf and resort amenities close to home
  • Freeway access for commutes across the northeast Valley
  • Newer construction compared with Central Phoenix or older Scottsdale neighborhoods
