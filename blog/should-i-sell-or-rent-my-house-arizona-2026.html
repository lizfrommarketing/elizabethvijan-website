Should You Sell Your House or Rent It Out in Arizona? The 2026 Landlord Checklist

Should you sell your house or rent it out? Rent it if the rent covers your full costs, you have a low-rate mortgage worth keeping, and you're ready for landlord duties. Sell if you need the equity, want a clean break, or want to protect the IRS home sale exclusion, which requires 24 months of ownership and use in the 5 years before the sale.

What's different in Arizona in 2026? Two things work in a landlord's favor and two add paperwork. Cities no longer charge rental sales tax on long-term residential rentals (since Jan. 1, 2025). But you must register the rental with the county assessor, and a registered rental loses a property tax credit that owner-occupied homes get.

Data note: Tax exclusion rules come from IRS Publication 523. Registration rules come from A.R.S. 33-1902 and the Maricopa County Assessor. Rental TPT changes come from the Arizona Department of Revenue, and deposit limits from A.R.S. 33-1321. General information, not tax or legal advice.

Start With the Math

Compare monthly rent against your true monthly cost:

  • Mortgage payment (principal, interest, taxes, insurance)
  • HOA dues, if any
  • Landlord insurance, which differs from a homeowner policy; see homeowners insurance costs in Arizona
  • Repairs and reserves for AC, roof and appliances
  • Vacancy between tenants
  • Management fees if you hire a property manager

For a rough local benchmark, the Census Bureau's 2020-2024 American Community Survey puts median gross rent at $2,013 in Scottsdale and $1,582 in Phoenix. Your home's rent will depend on its size, condition and location, so get a rental analysis for your address.

The Tax Trade-Off

Selling Your Main Home (IRS Pub. 523)

  • Exclude up to $250,000 of gain, or $500,000 for married couples filing jointly
  • You must have owned and lived in the home at least 24 months in the 5 years before the sale
  • Rent it too long and you can lose eligibility. Know your date.

Renting It First

  • Depreciation taken after May 6, 1997 can't be excluded; it's recaptured when you sell
  • Keep careful records of rent, expenses and improvements
  • More on Arizona's side: Arizona capital gains tax on a home sale

Arizona Landlord Requirements

Register the Rental (A.R.S. 33-1902)

  • Register with the county assessor: owner name, address, phone, property address and parcel, and year built
  • Update within 10 days of any change
  • Out-of-state owners must name an Arizona statutory agent
  • The statute says the property shall not be occupied if the information isn't on file

Expect a Higher Tax Bill (Maricopa County Assessor)

  • A rental to non-family tenants is Legal Class 4.2
  • Registration doesn't change the assessment ratio, but Class 4.2 rentals don't get the State Aid to Education Tax Credit, which may increase your bill
  • If a qualified family member lives there as a primary residence, it's Class 3.2

Rental Sales Tax Is Gone for Long-Term Rentals (ADOR)

  • City residential rental TPT (stays of 30 days or more) ended Jan. 1, 2025
  • Licenses with only residential rental activity were canceled automatically
  • Short-term rentals are different; see Scottsdale short-term rental rules

Security Deposits (A.R.S. 33-1321)

  • Maximum 1.5 months' rent, though tenants may voluntarily prepay rent
  • The purpose of any nonrefundable fee must be stated in writing
  • After move-out and a tenant's demand: an itemized list of deductions within 14 days, excluding weekends and legal holidays

Questions to Settle Before You Decide

  • Does your mortgage or HELOC allow renting? Check your loan documents
  • Does your HOA limit rentals or lease terms?
  • Will you self-manage or hire a manager?
  • When does your 2-of-5-year window close?
  • If you'll buy your next home with a new loan, how will the lender count this one? See sell before you buy?

FAQ: Selling vs. Renting in Arizona

Should I sell my house or rent it out?

Rent if the numbers and your appetite for landlording work; sell if you need equity or want to protect the exclusion.

Do I have to register a rental property in Arizona?

Yes, with the county assessor, under A.R.S. 33-1902.

Do I still pay rental sales tax in Scottsdale or Phoenix?

Not on long-term rentals since Jan. 1, 2025.

How much can an Arizona landlord charge for a security deposit?

Up to 1.5 months' rent.
