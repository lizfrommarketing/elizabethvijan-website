Chandler Tumbleweed Tree Lighting 2026: Dec. 5 Parade of Lights Times, Route, Parking and Street Closures

When is the Chandler Tumbleweed Tree lighting? Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, in downtown Chandler: festivities from 4:30 p.m., the Parade of Lights at 6:45 p.m., and the tree lighting at about 8 p.m. Admission is free. All of this year's celebrations are on the west side of Dr. A.J. Chandler Park.

Why this year is special: the City of Chandler says the Tumbleweed Tree debuted in 1957, making 2026 its 70th year, and the event doubles as the first public look at the park's renovated west side.

Data note: Event details are from the City of Chandler news release of Nov. 10, 2026. Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Update for Chandler, October 2026. Read November 17, 2026. Confirm details with the city before you go.

Tumbleweed Tree Lighting 2026 at a Glance

  • Date: Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026
  • Where: west side of Dr. A.J. Chandler Park, downtown Chandler
  • Festivities: 4:30-6:30 p.m., with games and prizes, photos with Santa and carolers
  • Parade of Lights: 6:45 p.m.
  • Tree lighting: about 8 p.m., after the parade
  • Cost: free
  • On display: through Jan. 3, 2027

Five Facts About the Tree

From the City of Chandler:

  • Size: about 34 feet tall and 20 feet wide
  • Materials: hundreds of tumbleweeds, more than 150 pounds of glitter, 25 gallons of white paint and 20 gallons of fire retardant
  • Lights: more than 2,000 LEDs
  • Fame: featured on several Travel Channel shows and Saturday Night Live
  • History: debuted in 1957

Holiday Festivities

  • Santa's House: free photos near the tree, closed 6:30-8 p.m. while Santa rides in the parade
  • Letters to Santa and Holiday Magic displays, including a glowing light tunnel, a candy sleigh and a giant walk-through gift box
  • Toy drive: the Chandler Fire Department collects new, unwrapped toys from 4:30-9 p.m. on the north side of the parking lot in front of San Marcos Resort, 1 N. San Marcos Place

How to Watch the Parade

  • Route: north on Arizona Avenue between Frye Road and Erie Street
  • Entries: more than 50, including floats with thousands of lights, dancers and community groups
  • Finale: Santa arrives on a red fire truck
  • Tip: the city suggests bringing chairs and blankets and arriving early; dinner downtown first makes it easy to hold a spot

Parking and Street Closures

Parking: free, in multiple garages and surface lots, including five garages on the east and west sides of Arizona Avenue. The city calls the Oregon Street garage the largest.

Scheduled closures on Dec. 5:

  • 5 a.m.-midnight: San Marcos Place, Boston to Buffalo streets
  • 2-8:30 p.m.: Arizona Avenue, Frye to Pecos roads
  • 2-10 p.m.: Arizona Avenue, Frye Road to Chandler Boulevard
  • 6-8:30 p.m.: Frye Road restricted, Washington to California streets
  • 6-9 p.m.: Chandler Boulevard restricted, Delaware to California streets
  • 6-9:30 p.m.: Arizona Avenue, Galveston Street to Chandler Boulevard

Smaller downtown streets may also close or be restricted.

A Simple Plan for the Evening

Here's one way to build the night around the city's schedule:

  1. Arrive by about 4:30 p.m. for the festivities, and plan your route around afternoon closures, since parts of Arizona Avenue close as early as 2 p.m.
  2. Visit Santa's House early, since it closes from 6:30 to 8 p.m. while Santa rides in the parade
  3. Drop off a toy at the Fire Department booth near San Marcos Resort
  4. Grab dinner downtown, as the city suggests, then claim your spot on Arizona Avenue
  5. Watch the parade at 6:45 p.m., then stay for the lighting at about 8 p.m.
  6. Can't make Dec. 5? The tree stays up through Jan. 3, 2027, so a quieter weeknight visit works too
