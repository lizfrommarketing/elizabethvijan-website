Scottsdale Weather in November: Average Temperatures, Rain and What to Expect

What's the weather like in Scottsdale in November? Mild and mostly dry. NOAA's 1991-2020 normals for Scottsdale Airport show an average high of 74.8°F, an average low of 50.6°F and 0.79 inch of rain for the whole month. It's the month the Valley shifts from warm afternoons to cool, crisp evenings.

How does early November compare with late November? Early November still averages more warm days, with about 10 days of 80°F or higher across the month on average. By the end of the month, highs slide toward December's normal of 64.6°F, and lows head toward 42.5°F.

Data note: Temperatures and precipitation are NOAA NCEI 1991-2020 climate normals for Scottsdale Airport (station USW00003192) unless noted. Normals are long-term averages; any given year can run warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.

November at a Glance (Scottsdale Airport Normals)

  • Average high: 74.8°F
  • Average low: 50.6°F
  • Monthly mean: 62.7°F
  • Normal precipitation: 0.79 inch
  • Days with 0.10 inch of rain or more: about 1.3
  • Days 80°F or warmer: about 10.4
  • Days 90°F or warmer: about 1
  • Nights 40°F or colder: about 1.4
  • Nights at or below freezing: about 0.2

Phoenix for Comparison

At Phoenix Sky Harbor, November normals are a 76.5°F high, a 53.7°F low and 0.57 inch of rain. Scottsdale's airport, farther north, typically runs a little cooler.

October, November and December Side by Side

  • October: high 87.3°F, low 62.7°F
  • November: high 74.8°F, low 50.6°F
  • December: high 64.6°F, low 42.5°F, 0.99 inch of rain

That 12-plus-degree drop in average highs from October to November is why locals notice the change, and why patios, trails and golf courses fill up.

Rain in November

November is one of the drier months, but not a dry one:

  • Normal rainfall: 0.79 inch at Scottsdale Airport, rising to 0.99 inch in December
  • Rainy days: only about 1.3 days a month reach a tenth of an inch
  • Phoenix Sky Harbor: a lower 0.57 inch normal for November

When a storm does come through, it's usually a good day for indoor plans. Washes and low-water road crossings can run after heavy rain, so follow any posted closures.

What to Pack or Plan

  • Layers: a light jacket for evenings, especially after sunset
  • Sun protection: the sun is still strong on clear afternoons
  • Outdoor time: hiking, patio dining and festivals are in season. See our November 2026 Scottsdale events guide
  • Clocks: Arizona doesn't observe daylight saving time, so the time difference with other states shifts in early November. Our daylight saving guide explains

City Holidays to Note

The City of Scottsdale lists Veterans Day (Wed., Nov. 11), Thanksgiving (Thu., Nov. 26) and the day after Thanksgiving (Fri., Nov. 27) as observed holidays. Check hours for city facilities on those dates.

Can You Swim in November?

  • Air: many afternoons are warm enough for pool time
  • Water: unheated pools cool as nights fall toward 50°F
  • Public pools: the City of Scottsdale posts lap swim availability for Cactus, Chaparral, Eldorado and McDowell Mountain Ranch aquatic centers. Resident daily lap swim is $4 adult / $3 child, per the city fee schedule

When Does It Freeze?

NOAA's normals for Scottsdale Airport put the first freeze (32°F) at:

  • 10% chance by Dec. 4
  • 50% chance (median) by Dec. 26
  • 90% chance by Jan. 20

Freezing nights average 1.4 in December. If you garden, plan frost cover for December, not November.

Why November Is a Great Month to House Hunt

  • Comfortable showing weather for walking neighborhoods, yards and pools
  • Outdoor living is on display: patios, fire pits and mountain views at their best
  • Seasonal residents return, so it's a good time to see how a neighborhood feels in its busiest months
