Fountain Hills vs. Scottsdale: What Living in Fountain Hills, AZ Is Really Like in 2026

Is Fountain Hills a good alternative to Scottsdale? For many buyers, yes, and price is the first reason. Phoenix REALTORS® data show a year-to-date median single-family price of $893,500 in Fountain Hills through September 2026, versus $1,250,000 in Scottsdale. You trade some of Scottsdale's shopping, dining and nightlife for a smaller town center and a community that has made protecting its night skies part of its identity.

What is Fountain Hills known for? Its namesake fountain, which rises to 330 feet on a typical day and runs 15 minutes every hour from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., plus its status as the world's 17th International Dark Sky Community. Here's how the two compare.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS® Local Market Updates for Fountain Hills and Scottsdale, September 2026. Town facts come from the Town of Fountain Hills, Experience Fountain Hills, DarkSky International and the International Dark Sky Discovery Center (checked Oct. 22, 2026).

Fountain Hills vs. Scottsdale: The 2026 Market Side by Side

Single-Family Homes

  • YTD median price (through Sept.): Fountain Hills $893,500 (+2.1%) · Scottsdale $1,250,000 (+5.5%)
  • September median: Fountain Hills $754,500 (-17.7%) · Scottsdale $1,200,000 (+4.3%)
  • Months of supply: Fountain Hills 4.5 · Scottsdale 3.7
  • Days on market (Sept.): Fountain Hills 82 · Scottsdale 80
  • Percent of list price received (Sept.): Fountain Hills 96.9% · Scottsdale 97.2%
  • Pending sales (Sept.): Fountain Hills 9, down 75% · Scottsdale 192, down 40.2%

Townhomes and Condos

  • YTD median price: Fountain Hills $375,000 (flat) · Scottsdale $495,000 (-1.0%)
  • Months of supply (Sept.): Fountain Hills 6.5 · Scottsdale 5.7

A word of caution on Fountain Hills' September numbers: with only 26 single-family closings, one month can swing hard. The year-to-date median is the steadier read. Both markets saw pending sales fall sharply in September, which typically shows up in closings over the following months.

Life in Fountain Hills: The Highlights

The Fountain and Fountain Park

  • Built in 1970 by developer Robert McCulloch, who also brought London Bridge to Lake Havasu City
  • Height: 330 feet for everyday viewing, 560 feet at full height on special occasions
  • Schedule: 15 minutes at the top of every hour, 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., daily; it may shut off in winds above about 10 mph
  • Fountain Park: 64 acres with a 29-acre lake, an 18-hole disc golf course, a seasonal splash pad and 36 pieces of the Town's public art collection

Dark Skies

  • Named the world's 17th International Dark Sky Community by DarkSky International in 2018
  • The International Dark Sky Discovery Center says it opens in early 2027, with a 27.5-inch PlaneWave telescope, which it calls the largest in Greater Phoenix

Festivals and Town Life

  • Fall Fountain Festival of Fine Arts & Crafts: Nov. 13-15, 2026, free, along Avenue of the Fountains and Saguaro Boulevard
  • Turkey Trot 5K: the 36th annual run returns Thanksgiving morning
  • A planned town: established in 1970 on former ranch land and designed by Charles Wood Jr., whose résumé includes Disneyland

How Fountain Hills Differs From Scottsdale

  • Scale: a small-town center versus Scottsdale's Old Town, Kierland and Scottsdale Quarter
  • Price per entry point: lower medians in both single-family and condo segments
  • Setting: a planned town organized around Fountain Park and its lake
  • ZIP code: Fountain Hills is 85268; Scottsdale spans several ZIP codes, including 85250 around Chaparral Park

If you're weighing north Scottsdale against Fountain Hills, compare commute routes and HOA rules early. Both vary widely street to street.

FAQ: Fountain Hills, AZ

Is Fountain Hills cheaper than Scottsdale?

By median price, yes: $893,500 vs. $1,250,000 YTD for single-family homes (Phoenix REALTORS®, Sept. 2026).

What time does the Fountain Hills fountain go off?

15 minutes at the top of every hour, 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., daily, weather permitting.

Is Fountain Hills a Dark Sky community?

Yes, the world's 17th (2018). A discovery center is slated for early 2027.

What ZIP code is Fountain Hills, AZ?

85268, in Maricopa County.
