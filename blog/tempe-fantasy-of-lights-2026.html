Tempe Fantasy of Lights 2026: Street Parade and Tree Lighting Nov. 28, Boat Parade Dec. 12 on Tempe Town Lake

When is Tempe's Fantasy of Lights in 2026? The Street Parade and Tree Lighting is Saturday, Nov. 28, on Mill Avenue, and the Boat Parade is Saturday, Dec. 12, on Tempe Town Lake. Both are free. The boat parade night opens at 4 p.m. at Tempe Beach Park, boats set out around 7 p.m., and fireworks are scheduled for 8:15 p.m., according to Downtown Tempe.

Why it's worth the drive: Tempe Tourism says the boat parade began in 1999 and calls it one of the most unique holiday events in metro Phoenix, with lighted boats of all shapes and sizes circling the lake to holiday music. Here's the schedule, the parade route and the easiest ways to get there.

Data note: Event details are from Downtown Tempe's official Fantasy of Lights pages and Tempe Tourism. Market data is from Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime, Tempe, October 2026. Read November 16, 2026. Confirm times before you go.

Fantasy of Lights 2026 at a Glance

  • Street Parade + Tree Lighting: Sat., Nov. 28, 4-8 p.m., Downtown Tempe; parade at 6 p.m.
  • Boat Parade: Sat., Dec. 12, 4-9 p.m., Tempe Beach Park, 80 W. Rio Salado Parkway
  • Cost: free
  • Bags: clear bag policy at the boat parade
  • Nearby: Tempe Festival of the Arts, Dec. 4-6, on Mill Avenue

Street Parade + Tree Lighting (Nov. 28)

Downtown Tempe calls this the official start of its holiday season.

Schedule

  • 4 p.m.: CenterPoint Plaza opens with DJ Nasdak and emcee Arizona Theatre Company; snack and drink vendors line Mill Avenue
  • 4-5:30 p.m.: the Marcos de Niza Chamber Singers carol the streets
  • 6 p.m.: the parade steps off at Mill Avenue and 3rd Street
  • After the parade: the 36-foot holiday tree lights up at CenterPoint Plaza, with free photos with Santa and a letter-writing station

The route

From 3rd Street and Mill Avenue, south on Mill, west on 7th Street through CenterPoint Plaza, north on Maple Avenue, ending at 5th Street between Maple and Ash. Viewing is first come, first served.

Same night elsewhere: Scottsdale lights its tree at Scottsdazzle on Nov. 28, too.

Boat Parade on Tempe Town Lake (Dec. 12)

Schedule

  • 4 p.m.: Tempe Beach Park opens with a holiday market, Santa photos, letters to the North Pole, DJ Nasdak, food vendors and beer and wine gardens
  • 7 p.m.: boat parade begins
  • 8:15 p.m.: fireworks finale
  • 9 p.m.: event ends

What to expect

  • Boats decked in holiday lights, inflatables and creative themes
  • A food truck lineup spanning fry bread, Hawaiian barbecue, crepes, churros and pizza
  • A crowd of thousands, per Downtown Tempe; arrive early for a lakeside spot

Getting There

  • Light rail: Valley Metro offers free parking at 13 park-and-ride lots; exit at Mill Avenue and 3rd Street
  • Tempe Streetcar: 14 stops on a 3-mile route, every 15-20 minutes; use the Tempe Beach Park/Rio Salado or Hayden Ferry/Rio Salado stops
  • Driving: special-event rates at select garages and lots; check Tempe TRiP for real-time availability
  • From Scottsdale: south Scottsdale borders Tempe, and the light rail park-and-rides on Apache Boulevard (Dorsey Lane, McClintock Road and Price-101) are an easy alternative to downtown parking

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Tempe Fantasy of Lights Boat Parade 2026?

Saturday, Dec. 12. Park opens at 4 p.m., boats at 7 p.m., fireworks at 8:15 p.m.

When is the Tempe Fantasy of Lights Street Parade and Tree Lighting?

Saturday, Nov. 28, 4-8 p.m.; parade at 6 p.m., then the tree lighting at CenterPoint Plaza.

Where is the best place to watch the Tempe boat parade?

Tempe Beach Park, from 4 p.m. Arrive early and bring a clear bag.

Where do you park for the Tempe Fantasy of Lights?

Downtown garages at event rates, or light rail and the Tempe Streetcar.
