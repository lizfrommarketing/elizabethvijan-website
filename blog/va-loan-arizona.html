VA Loan Arizona: Requirements, Funding Fee and Loan Limits for Scottsdale and Phoenix Buyers in 2026

How does a VA loan work in Arizona? The Department of Veterans Affairs guarantees part of a private lender's loan, which in most cases lets eligible Veterans, service members and some surviving spouses buy a primary home with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. You'll need a Certificate of Eligibility (COE), and you'll still qualify with a lender on credit, income, debts and assets.

What it costs: most borrowers pay a one-time VA funding fee of 1.25% to 3.3% of the loan, which can be financed. Many Veterans with service-connected disability compensation are exempt. With full entitlement, the VA sets no loan limit.

VA Loan Requirements

Service

VA.gov lists minimum active-duty service by period. Examples:

  • Current service members: at least 90 continuous days
  • Veterans who served Aug. 2, 1990 to present: generally 24 continuous months, or the full period (at least 90 days) you were called to active duty
  • National Guard and Reserve: options include 90 days of qualifying active-duty service or six creditable years of service
  • Exceptions: shorter service can qualify if you were discharged for a service-connected disability

The home and the loan

  • Primary residence: VA.gov says you must live in the home you buy with the loan
  • Credit: the VA doesn't set a minimum score; lenders may
  • Appraisal cap: the maximum VA loan is the lower of the appraised value or the purchase price

The VA Funding Fee in 2026

Purchase and construction loans, as a share of the loan amount:

  • First use: 2.15% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% or more, 1.25% with 10% or more
  • After first use: 3.3% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% or more, 1.25% with 10% or more
  • Cash-out refinance: 2.15% first use, 3.3% after
  • IRRRL (rate-reduction refinance): 0.5%

You can finance the fee into the loan or pay it at closing. The VA's own example: a first-time user buying a $200,000 home with $10,000 down pays 1.5% of the $190,000 loan, or $2,850.

Who doesn't pay

VA.gov lists exemptions including Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, those eligible for compensation but receiving retirement or active-duty pay, certain surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, and active-duty Purple Heart recipients.

VA Loan Limits and Entitlement

  • Full entitlement: your COE shows basic entitlement of $36,000, and the VA says you don't have a loan limit, provided you qualify and the appraisal supports the price
  • How the guaranty works: on loans over $144,000, the VA guarantees up to 25% of the loan amount
  • Partial entitlement: your no-down-payment ceiling is based on the county loan limit, which matches FHFA's conforming limit. FHFA's 2026 baseline one-unit limit is $832,750, which applies in most of the country, including Maricopa County.

That matters in Scottsdale, where Phoenix REALTORS reports a single-family median sales price of $1,225,000 in October 2026.

VA Loans in Today's Market

  • Benchmark rate: Freddie Mac's 30-year conventional average was 7.11% on Nov. 12, 2026. VA rates are set by lenders, so compare quotes.
  • Inventory: Scottsdale had 1,480 single-family homes for sale in October, a 4.2-month supply, giving VA buyers room to negotiate
  • Assumptions: the VA's fee chart lists a 0.5% funding fee on loan assumptions; see assumable mortgages in Arizona

How to Get Started

  1. Request your COE through VA.gov or your lender
  2. Get preapproved with at least two VA-approved lenders
  3. Check funding fee status, especially if you receive disability compensation
  4. Ask about property tax relief: see the Arizona disabled veteran property tax exemption

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use a VA loan twice?

Yes. Subsequent-use funding fees apply, and your remaining entitlement sets your no-down-payment limit.

What credit score do I need for a VA loan?

The VA has no minimum, but lenders may. Compare more than one lender.

Is there a VA loan limit in Arizona for 2026?

Not with full entitlement. With partial entitlement, the county limit applies; Maricopa's 2026 one-unit limit is $832,750.

Who is exempt from the VA funding fee?

Veterans receiving service-connected disability compensation, among others listed on VA.gov.
