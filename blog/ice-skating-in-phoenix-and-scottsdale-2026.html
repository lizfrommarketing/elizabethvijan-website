Ice Skating in Phoenix and Scottsdale 2026: Outdoor Holiday Rinks, Dates, Hours and Tips

Where can you go ice skating in Phoenix and Scottsdale this holiday season? The two big outdoor rinks are Desert Ice at Christmas at the Princess in Scottsdale (Nov. 20, 2026 to Jan. 2, 2027) and Frostlight Isle at JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge (Nov. 18, 2026 to Jan. 3, 2027). For year-round indoor skating, Ice Den Scottsdale runs public sessions with advance online registration.

Timing tip: Frostlight Isle opens today, Nov. 18, and the Princess opens Friday, Nov. 20, so early weeknights are the easiest way to skate before the holiday crowds peak.

Data note: Dates, hours and rules are from each venue's official website: Christmas at the Princess (Fairmont Scottsdale Princess), JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge tickets, and Ice Den Scottsdale. Read November 18, 2026. Confirm before you go, since hours can change for weather or private events.

Desert Ice at Christmas at the Princess (Scottsdale)

The Fairmont Scottsdale Princess, 7575 E. Princess Drive, turns its grounds into one of the Valley's biggest holiday events.

  • Dates: Nov. 20, 2026 through Jan. 2, 2027
  • Hours: Monday to Thursday 5 to 10 p.m.; Friday to Sunday 4 to 11 p.m.; daily 4 to 11 p.m. beginning Dec. 16
  • The rink: a 6,000-square-foot outdoor rink with real ice
  • Ice Pass: includes skate rental and unlimited skating for the evening, in one-hour sessions based on capacity and availability
  • Skate sizes: junior size 8 to adult size 15, plus adjustable double-bladed skates that attach over children's shoes
  • Ability rule: skaters must stand and skate on their own; skating walkers and assistance aren't available

Beyond the ice, the event lists 17 million lights, a 120-foot Ferris wheel and 65 acres of displays. Admission and Ice Passes are sold online; see our full Christmas at the Princess 2026 guide.

Frostlight Isle at JW Marriott Phoenix Desert Ridge (North Phoenix)

The resort's holiday season runs Nov. 18, 2026 through Jan. 3, 2027, with a lakeside rink surrounded by lights.

  • Hours: Sunday to Thursday 5 to 9 p.m.; Friday and Saturday 5 to 10 p.m.
  • Tickets: purchased online in advance; there's no on-site box office
  • What's included: unlimited skating on your ticket date, as long as you keep your wristband on
  • Kids: children 11 and younger must be accompanied by a ticketed adult on the ice
  • Help on the ice: a limited number of skating assist devices, first come, first served
  • Parking: additional fees apply

The resort also debuts ice!, an all-new attraction with colossal ice sculptures of scenes from Elf.

Ice Den Scottsdale (Indoor, Year-Round)

If you'd rather skate indoors:

  • Public skating: two-hour sessions on select days and times
  • Registration: advance online registration required
  • Thanksgiving week: public skate on select dates, Nov. 24 through 29
  • Lessons: Learn to Skate classes for all ages and levels

Ice Den is also home to the Jr. Coyotes youth hockey program, so check the public-session calendar before you go.

Tips for a Great Night on the Ice

  • Book early for Fridays and Saturdays in December, especially at the resorts
  • Dress in layers: desert evenings cool off quickly after sunset; see Scottsdale weather in November
  • Wear thick socks and bring gloves for little ones
  • Budget for parking at the resorts, which is sold separately
  • Make a night of it: pair skating with Phoenix Zoo ZooLights or Glendale Glitters on another evening
“Skating at the Princess is one of my favorite December traditions, and it surprises every client who's just moved here from a snowy state. My advice is to go on a Monday or Tuesday early in the season, skate first, then walk the lights. You get the magic without the crowds, and the desert night air is perfect for it.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I go ice skating outdoors in Phoenix or Scottsdale?

Desert Ice at the Princess (Nov. 20 to Jan. 2) and Frostlight Isle at JW Desert Ridge (Nov. 18 to Jan. 3).

Is there ice skating at the Scottsdale Princess?

Yes. A 6,000-square-foot real-ice rink with skate rental included in the Ice Pass.

Do I need tickets in advance for ice skating at JW Desert Ridge?

Yes. Tickets are sold online only, with no on-site box office.

Is there indoor ice skating in Scottsdale?

Yes. Ice Den Scottsdale offers public sessions with advance online registration.
