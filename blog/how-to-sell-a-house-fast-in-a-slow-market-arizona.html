How to Sell a House Fast in a Slow Market: 9 Smart Moves for Phoenix and Scottsdale Sellers in Late 2026

How do you sell a house fast in a slow market? Price it right on day one, make it inspection-ready, market it like a luxury listing and give buyers a reason to act, such as a closing-cost credit or a rate buydown. Speed in a slower market comes from removing every reason a buyer might wait.

Why it matters right now: Phoenix REALTORS reports 16,708 single-family homes for sale in Maricopa County in October 2026, up 11.6% from a year earlier, with 4.3 months of supply. Homes averaged 71 days to an accepted offer, and pending sales were down sharply year over year. Buyers have choices. Here's how to make yours the one they pick.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Update for Maricopa County, October 2026, and the Housing Supply Overview for October 2026 (rolling 12-month figures; both current as of Nov. 3, 2026; ARMLS data). Mortgage rates are from Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey, Nov. 5, 2026. Read November 12, 2026.

The Market You're Selling Into (Maricopa County, October 2026)

Single-family homes:

  • Homes for sale: 16,708, up 11.6%
  • Months supply: 4.3, up from 3.8
  • Days on market until sale: 71 (listing to accepted offer)
  • Median sales price: $499,990, essentially flat
  • Percent of list price received: 98.0%
  • Pending sales: 2,147, down 46.2% from October 2025

Region-wide, rolling 12 months:

  • Fastest price range: $375,000-$474,999 at 75 days
  • Slowest price range: $633,000 and up at 83 days
  • New construction: 89 days vs. 78 for previously owned homes, so you're also competing with builders

9 Moves That Speed Up a Sale

1. Price to the market, not to last year

Buyers compare your home to everything else online. Price in line with recent closed sales, not old peaks or neighbors' hopes. In my experience, overpricing is the most common reason a home sits, and a later reduction rarely recaptures the first weeks of attention. Our guide to what it costs to sell in Arizona helps you plan your real net.

2. Aim just under a search threshold

Many buyers search in round-number brackets. Pricing at $599,000 rather than $605,000 can put you in front of more of them.

3. Get ahead of the inspection

A pre-listing inspection lets you fix or disclose issues on your terms. Buyers in a slower market are quick to walk from surprises.

4. Fix the small things, skip the big remodel

Paint touch-ups, lighting, landscaping and a deep clean usually do more for speed than a new kitchen you won't recoup.

5. Invest in presentation

Professional photography, a floor plan and video tour matter most when buyers have more listings to scroll through. Staging, even partially, helps buyers see scale.

6. Offer what buyers need: payment relief

With the 30-year average at 7.18%, a seller credit toward a rate buydown or closing costs can do more for a buyer's monthly payment than a similar price cut. See our comparison of a rate buydown vs. a price reduction.

7. Make showings easy

Lockbox access, flexible hours and a home that's always "show ready" capture the buyers who are touring this weekend, not next month.

8. Be flexible on terms

A quick close, a rent-back or a closing date that matches the buyer's lease can tip a decision your way.

9. Watch the first two weeks closely

If showings are thin or feedback repeats the same objection, adjust early. A new listing gets its freshest look from buyers in its first weekends, so don't let a fixable problem linger.

What About Cash Buyers?

Home-buying companies can close quickly, but speed usually comes at a discount. Ask for the offer in writing, compare it against a realistic listing net, and read every contingency. For comparison, Maricopa County single-family sellers received 98.0% of list price on average in October. If you're also buying, read should I sell my house before buying another.

“The fastest sales I've had in slow markets weren't the cheapest homes; they were the ones that looked like the best value the day they hit the market. Sellers who let me price it right, polish it and offer a buyer a payment solution are usually signing a contract while the competition is still talking about price cuts.”

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you sell a house fast in a slow market?

Price to recent sales, prepare for inspection, present it professionally and offer buyer incentives like a rate buydown.

How long does it take to sell a house in a buyer's market?

Maricopa County single-family homes averaged 71 days to an accepted offer in October 2026, plus escrow time.

Is it better to lower the price or offer concessions?

It depends on the buyer; with rates above 7%, payment-lowering credits often stand out.

Should I sell my house for cash to a home-buying company?

It can be faster but often at a discount. Compare the net carefully.
