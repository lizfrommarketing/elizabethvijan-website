How Long Does a Mortgage Pre-Approval Last? Expiration, Credit Impact and Pre-Qualified vs. Pre-Approved for Arizona Buyers

How long does a mortgage pre-approval last? There's no universal expiration date: each lender sets its own, so the date printed on your letter is the one that counts. In practice, letters go stale because the documents behind them do. For conventional loans sold to Fannie Mae, credit reports and income, employment and asset documents can be no more than four months old on the note date.

The good news: refreshing a pre-approval is usually quick, and shopping several lenders won't wreck your credit. The CFPB says mortgage credit checks within 45 days count as one inquiry.

Data note: Pre-approval and credit guidance are from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. Document-age rules are from Fannie Mae Selling Guide B1-1-03 (dated April 2, 2025). Rates are from Freddie Mac's PMMS. Prices are from Phoenix REALTORS (October 2026). Read November 19, 2026.

Why Pre-Approvals Expire

A pre-approval is a snapshot of your finances on the day the lender reviewed them. Fannie Mae's Selling Guide B1-1-03 spells out why that snapshot ages:

  • Credit documents include credit reports and employment, income and asset documentation
  • They must be no more than four months old on the note date, the day you sign your loan
  • When documents are older than allowed, the lender must update them

So even if your letter has no expiration printed on it, a search that stretches past a few months will mean new pay stubs, new statements and possibly a new credit pull before closing.

Rates move too

A pre-approval amount assumes a rate. Freddie Mac's PMMS put the 30-year fixed average at 7.11% on Nov. 12, 2026, down from 7.18% a week earlier. Even small moves change your maximum price, which is another reason to refresh before you write an offer.

Does Getting Pre-Approved Hurt Your Credit?

From the CFPB:

  • Lenders may check your credit for a prequalification or preapproval
  • An inquiry typically has a small negative effect on your scores
  • Multiple mortgage credit checks within 45 days are recorded as a single inquiry
  • Checking your own credit does not affect your scores

Avoid new credit during the process. The CFPB specifically warns against applying for credit cards, car loans or other credit right before or during the mortgage process. If your credit needs work first, see what credit score you need to buy a house.

Pre-Qualified vs. Pre-Approved: What Sellers Actually See

The CFPB's answer may surprise you: lenders use the words differently.

  • Some lenders issue a prequalification based on unverified information you report
  • Some issue a preapproval only after verifying your information
  • Some offer a written commitment letter, valid for a set period, subject to limited conditions
  • Neither is a guaranteed loan offer

The CFPB's advice: don't worry about the label; ask what was verified. That matters in competitive situations, where a fully documented approval reads stronger to a seller than a quick online estimate.

How to Keep Your Pre-Approval Fresh

  1. Note the date on your letter and your lender's refresh policy
  2. Keep documents current: latest pay stubs, two recent bank statements, tax returns
  3. Don't open new credit or make large purchases on credit
  4. Avoid large unexplained deposits without documentation
  5. Update your letter for each offer so the amount fits the home

Before you shop, run the numbers in how much house can I afford and check your debt-to-income ratio.

What Pre-Approval Means at Today's Prices

  • Phoenix median single-family price, October 2026: $481,998
  • Scottsdale median single-family price, October 2026: $1,225,000

At those levels, a stale letter can easily be tens of thousands of dollars out of step with what a lender would approve today.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a mortgage pre-approval good for?

Until the date your lender sets. Fannie Mae requires credit documents no more than four months old at closing.

Does a pre-approval affect your credit score?

Slightly, but mortgage checks within 45 days count as one inquiry.

What is the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

Lenders use the terms differently; ask what was verified.

What happens if my pre-approval expires?

Your lender updates it with fresh documents.
