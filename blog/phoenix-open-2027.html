WM Phoenix Open 2027: Dates, Ticket Prices, Free Days, Birds Nest Lineup and Tips for Tournament Week

When is the 2027 Phoenix Open? The 92nd WM Phoenix Open is Feb. 8-14, 2027, at TPC Scottsdale. Tournament rounds run Thursday, Feb. 11 through Sunday, Feb. 14, and general admission is free on Monday, Feb. 8 and Tuesday, Feb. 9 thanks to Ford Free Days. Daily tickets are $75 plus fees Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday and $125 plus fees Friday and Saturday.

The big nights: the Coors Light Birds Nest runs Feb. 10-13, closing with Calvin Harris on Saturday, and the Concert in the Coliseum on the 16th hole features Imagine Dragons and Counting Crows on Saturday, Feb. 6. Here's everything locals need to plan the week.

Data note: Dates, prices, lineups and policies are from the WM Phoenix Open's official event schedule, ticket pages, spectator information and 2027 announcements. Read November 12, 2026. Details can change; confirm with the tournament before you go.

2027 WM Phoenix Open at a Glance

  • Dates: Feb. 8-14, 2027
  • Venue: TPC Scottsdale Stadium Course
  • Hosts: The Thunderbirds, who say the event has raised more than $246 million for charities
  • Free days: Monday, Feb. 8 and Tuesday, Feb. 9 (Ford Free Days)
  • Pro-Am: Annexus Pro-Am on Wednesday, Feb. 10
  • Rounds: Thursday-Sunday, Feb. 11-14
  • Kids: ages 15 and younger get in free

Ticket Prices for 2027

The tournament says general admission pricing held steady from last year:

  • Wednesday, Thursday, Sunday: $75 plus fees
  • Friday, Saturday: $125 plus fees
  • Monday, Tuesday: free
  • Kiva Club (covered venue between the 10th green and 17th tee): $200 plus fees Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday; $250 plus fees Friday and Saturday
  • Hospitality: daily and weekly packages at venues including Nine, Skybar, the 1937 Club and the Greenskeeper, while available

Military and first responders: The Thunderbirds offer complimentary admission to U.S. military (active, reserve, guard, veterans and retired) and active law enforcement, firefighters and EMTs, plus one guest, with a delivery fee. Registration opens in January, and each day's allocation is limited. Military members can also visit the Birdies for the Brave Patriots Outpost near the 18th tee.

The 2027 Concert Lineup

Coors Light Birds Nest (Feb. 10-13):

  • Wednesday, Feb. 10: Riley Green with special guest Ashley Cooke
  • Thursday, Feb. 11: Jason Aldean with special guest Josh Ross
  • Friday, Feb. 12: Snoop Dogg and T-Pain
  • Saturday, Feb. 13: Calvin Harris

Concert in the Coliseum (Saturday, Feb. 6): Imagine Dragons and Counting Crows on the famous 16th hole, with reserved seating and suites sold separately.

A Day-by-Day Game Plan

Monday and Tuesday: free and relaxed

Practice rounds and free admission make these the easiest days to bring kids or out-of-town guests and learn the course layout.

Wednesday: Pro-Am and the first Birds Nest night

Watch the pros play alongside their amateur partners, then stay for the opening Birds Nest night (separate ticket).

Thursday and Friday: championship golf

Rounds one and two. Thursday is priced at the lower $75 level; Friday moves to $125.

Saturday: Green Out and the biggest crowds

Saturday is Green Out: for every person who wears green, a donation goes to the tournament's sustainability fund. Saturday is a peak-priced day, so buy early and plan transportation ahead.

Sunday: the finish

Final round and trophy presentation, at the lower weekday price.

Parking and Getting There

The tournament says parking routes and lot availability may change as the event approaches and that the page will be updated through tournament week. Check the official Directions and Parking information and the 2027 course map before you go, and consider rideshare on Friday and Saturday. Remember the tournament's fan code of conduct: disruptive behavior can mean ejection.
