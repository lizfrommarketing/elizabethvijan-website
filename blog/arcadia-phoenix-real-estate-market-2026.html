Arcadia Phoenix Real Estate Market: Why the 85018 Median Jumped 30% in October 2026, and What It Means for Your Home

Are Arcadia home prices really up 30%? The October 2026 single-family median for ZIP 85018 rose 30.5% to $2,113,500, but that mostly reflects which homes sold, not a 30% jump in what a typical Arcadia home is worth. Closings nearly doubled, from 27 to 49, and the average sale price rose even faster, up 39.4% to $2,488,300.

The steadier number: For the year through October, the 85018 single-family median was $1,725,000, up 16.9%, on 387 closings. That's still a strong year, and a better guide for pricing most homes.

Data note: Figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Update for ZIP 85018, October 2026 (ARMLS data, current as of November 3, 2026), and the Zillow Observed Rent Index for 85018, October 2026. Listing and record-sale details are from KTAR reporting. Read November 23, 2026.

The October 2026 Numbers for 85018

Single-family

  • Median sale price: $2,113,500, up 30.5% from $1,620,000
  • Average sale price: $2,488,300, up 39.4%
  • Closed sales: 49, up from 27
  • Pending sales: 18, down from 30
  • Days on market until sale: 65
  • Percent of list price received: 95.9%
  • Inventory: 199 homes, 5.4 months of supply

Year to date through October

  • Median: $1,725,000, up 16.9%
  • Closed sales: 387, up 23.2%
  • Days on market: 79; 96.1% of list price received

Townhouse/condo, October

  • Median: $345,000, down 2.8%
  • Days on market: 111

Why the Median Jumped

When more expensive homes make up a bigger share of a month's sales, the median rises even if no individual home gained value. Three signs point to that in October:

  • Closings nearly doubled, giving the month a larger, more luxury-heavy mix
  • The average rose faster than the median, a sign of a heavier top end
  • The year-to-date median rose about half as much as the October figure

The Phoenix REALTORS report itself warns that small samples can make percent changes look extreme.

The Top of the Market Is Setting Headlines

  • The record: KTAR and the Phoenix Business Journal reported the $17.1 million cash sale of an estate on Exeter Boulevard in April 2026 as an Arcadia record. Its address is in Scottsdale, a reminder that the Arcadia name crosses city and ZIP lines
  • The next marker: A newly built 9,388-square-foot estate at 5626 E. Montecito Ave. was listed as a private sale for $23.5 million in September. KTAR noted it could set a new record if it sells near its $2,500-per-square-foot asking price

Those numbers are real, but they describe a small slice of 85018. The ZIP also includes the original ranch streets and the area many people call Arcadia Lite. For the difference, see Arcadia vs. Arcadia Lite.

What It Means If You're Selling

  • Price on your own comps. Use recent sales of similar size, lot, condition and location, not the October median
  • Expect negotiation. Sellers received 95.9% of list in October. On a $2 million listing, that gap is about $82,000
  • Plan for time. Homes took a median 65 days in October and 79 days year to date
  • Watch the pipeline. Pending sales fell 40% year over year in October, which may mean fewer closings in the months ahead

What It Means If You're Buying

  • More choice: 199 single-family homes on the market in October, up 8.7%
  • Room to negotiate, especially below the new-construction tier
  • Renting first? Zillow's typical rent for 85018 was about $1,611 in October 2026, down about 1.6% from a year earlier, reflecting the area's mix of apartments and condos

Mortgage rates matter at this price point too. Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed average was 7.28% on November 19, 2026. Loans above the conforming limit are jumbo loans; see jumbo loan limits in Arizona.
