Fiesta Bowl Parade 2027: Date, Start Time, Route, Parking and Bowl Week Guide for Phoenix and Scottsdale

When is the Fiesta Bowl Parade? The 54th annual Phoenix Children's Fiesta Bowl Parade presented by Lerner & Rowe steps off Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027, at 10 a.m. in Central Phoenix. It starts at North Central Avenue and Montebello Avenue and runs about 1.5 miles. It's free to watch, and it airs live on Arizona's Family 3TV.

What's new this year: Phoenix Children's is the parade's new title partner, and this time the parade closes bowl week rather than leading into the game as it did in January 2026, following the Fiesta Bowl's College Football Playoff Quarterfinal on Dec. 30. Here's everything you need to plan the morning.

Data note: 2026-27 details are from the Fiesta Sports Foundation's Oct. 7, 2026 announcement and parade page, and State Farm Stadium's Fiesta Bowl event page. Route, closure, parking and restroom details for the January 2026 parade are from Phoenix New Times (Jan. 2, 2026) and Arizona's Family (Jan. 2, 2026), shown as a guide only. Read November 11, 2026.

Fiesta Bowl Parade 2027: Quick Facts

  • Date: Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027
  • Start time: 10 a.m.
  • Start point: N. Central Ave. and Montebello Ave., Phoenix 85012
  • Length: about 1.5 miles
  • Cost: free for all attendees
  • Participants: more than 3,000 people and animals each year, per the foundation
  • Features: giant balloons, floats, marching bands, community groups and animals
  • TV: live on Arizona's Family 3TV, Arizona's Family Sports Network and digital platforms

The Route

The foundation has posted a 2026-27 route map on its parade page; check it for the final route.

For reference, the January 2026 parade:

  • Started at Central and Montebello avenues, just south of Bethany Home Road
  • Headed south on Central Avenue to Camelback Road
  • Turned east on Camelback toward Seventh Street
  • Ran from 10 a.m. to about 1 p.m.

Last year's street closures (6 a.m. to about 2 p.m.)

  • Central Ave. from Rose Lane to Camelback Road
  • Seventh St. from Colter St. to Indian School Road
  • Missouri Ave. and Bethany Home Road from Seventh Ave. to Seventh St.
  • Camelback Road from Seventh Ave. to 16th St.

Expect similar closures this year, and watch for City of Phoenix traffic advisories closer to the date.

Parking, Restrooms and Getting There

  • Parking: in January 2026, free street parking near the route was first-come, first-served, and nearby businesses and private lots offered paid parking
  • Restrooms: portable restrooms were placed along the route
  • Light rail: Valley Metro Rail has stations along Central Avenue near the route; check Valley Metro for parade-day service changes
  • From Scottsdale: arrive early and allow for closures that reached as far east as 16th Street last year

Best viewing tips

  • Every spot is first-come, first-served, so arrive early
  • Bring chairs, layers and water; January mornings start cool
  • If the crowds aren't your thing, watch the broadcast from home

The 2026-27 Bowl Week Schedule

The parade is the finale of seven days of bowl-season events:

  • Sat., Dec. 26: Cactus Bowl in Tempe
  • Wed., Dec. 30: Vrbo Fiesta Bowl, CFP Quarterfinal, 5:30 p.m. MST, State Farm Stadium, Glendale
  • Sat., Jan. 2: Phoenix Children's Fiesta Bowl Parade, Central Phoenix

Who's playing? The 12-team CFP field will be announced Sunday, Dec. 6, with first-round games Dec. 18-19. State Farm Stadium recommends buying parking in advance and notes its clear-bag policy.

Why Phoenix Children's

Phoenix Children's and the Fiesta Sports Foundation have partnered since 2011, with nearly $1 million in foundation contributions toward pediatric emergency and trauma care, inclusive play and patient experiences. This year's Phoenix Children's entry will feature a new custom float.
