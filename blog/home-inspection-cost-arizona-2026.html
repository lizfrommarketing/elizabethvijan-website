How Much Is a Home Inspection in Arizona? 2026 Costs, Timeline and What to Expect

How much is a home inspection in Arizona? Most cost $300 to $600 in 2026, according to Desert State Home Inspections' Arizona pricing guide, with price driven mainly by square footage. Homes over 5,000 square feet run $650 to $900+, and specialty add-ons like termite, pool and sewer scope inspections are priced separately.

When does it happen? After your offer is accepted, during the inspection period, which is 10 days by default in the Arizona REALTORS resale purchase contract. The buyer typically hires and pays the inspector, then uses the report to request repairs or credits through the BINSR (Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response).

Data note: Cost ranges come from a Phoenix-area inspection company's published 2026 guide and from cost site homeyou; actual quotes vary by home and provider. Licensing details come from the Arizona Board of Technical Registration and A.R.S. 32-122.02; contract terms from the Arizona REALTORS sample resale purchase contract (November 2025).

Arizona Home Inspection Cost by Home Size

Desert State Home Inspections' typical 2026 ranges for a standard buyer inspection:

  • Under 1,000 sq. ft.: $275-$350
  • 1,000-1,500 sq. ft.: $325-$400
  • 1,500-2,000 sq. ft.: $375-$450
  • 2,000-2,500 sq. ft.: $400-$500
  • 2,500-3,500 sq. ft.: $450-$550
  • 3,500-5,000 sq. ft.: $500-$650
  • 5,000+ sq. ft.: $650-$900+

For comparison, homeyou lists a typical Phoenix range of $193-$436 (October 2026). The differences highlight why you should get a quote for your specific home.

What Pushes the Price Up

  • Age: older homes take longer
  • Complexity: multi-story homes, casitas or guest houses, zoned HVAC
  • Luxury estates: Desert State notes larger Scottsdale and Paradise Valley homes often land at or above the top of these ranges

Common Add-On Inspections and Costs

  • Termite (WDI) inspection: $75-$125. Desert State notes many lenders require one for VA and FHA loans
  • Pool and spa inspection: $100-$200
  • Sewer scope: $150-$300, a camera inspection of the main sewer line
  • New construction phase inspections: $300-$500+ per phase
  • Pre-listing inspection (sellers): $300-$500

The Arizona Inspection Timeline

Under the Arizona REALTORS Contract

  • Inspection period: 10 days by default after acceptance
  • SPDS: the seller delivers the Seller's Property Disclosure Statement within 3 days
  • Termites: the buyer orders and pays for wood-destroying organism inspections during the inspection period
  • BINSR: the buyer's written notice to accept, cancel, or request repairs or credits

On Inspection Day

  • Expect 2 to 4 hours on site, per Desert State
  • Longer for larger or older homes and homes with pools or additional structures
  • Ask about report turnaround when you book so you stay inside your inspection period

For how inspections fit into the full escrow, see how long it takes to close on a house in Arizona. First-time buyers can also check our Scottsdale and Phoenix first-time buyer guide.

How Arizona Regulates Home Inspectors

  • Certification: required through the Arizona Board of Technical Registration
  • Requirements: National Home Inspector Examination, fingerprint clearance card, 84 classroom hours and 30 parallel inspections
  • Financial assurance: E&O insurance of $200,000 aggregate / $100,000 per occurrence, or a $25,000 bond (or proof of $25,000 in net assets) (A.R.S. 32-122.02)
  • Standards: inspections follow the Arizona ASHI Standards of Professional Practice adopted Oct. 27, 2023, with separate pool and spa standards
  • Scope: a visual, "picture in time" evaluation. Inspectors generally aren't contractors
  • Verify and complain: look up inspectors on the Board's online registry; the Board takes complaints but can't award damages

FAQ: Home Inspections in Arizona

How much is a home inspection in Arizona?

Most cost $300-$600, per Desert State's 2026 guide; homeyou lists $193-$436 in Phoenix.

Who pays for the home inspection in Arizona?

The buyer, typically, during the 10-day default inspection period.

How long does a home inspection take in Arizona?

About 2 to 4 hours, longer for large or older homes.

Do home inspectors need a license in Arizona?

Yes, certification through the Arizona Board of Technical Registration.
