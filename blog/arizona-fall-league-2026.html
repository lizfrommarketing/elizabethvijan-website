Arizona Fall League 2026: Schedule, Fall Stars Weekend, Tickets and Where to Catch a Game in Scottsdale

When is the Arizona Fall League? The 2026 season runs Oct. 3 through Nov. 14, and the best part is right now. Fall Stars Weekend lands at Salt River Fields at Talking Stick: the Home Run Derby on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 6:30 p.m., and the Fall Stars Game on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 6 p.m., live on MLB Network.

Can you still catch a game in Scottsdale? Yes. The Scottsdale Scorpions host games at Scottsdale Stadium on Wednesday, Nov. 4, and Friday, Nov. 6, both at 6:30 p.m. Individual tickets are $14 for adults, and kids 10 and under get in free with an adult.

Data note: Dates, times, teams and prices come from MLB's 2026 Arizona Fall League schedule, its August schedule announcement and the AFL tickets page. MLB notes all times are Arizona local time and subject to change. No market statistics are included in this post.

Arizona Fall League 2026 at a Glance

  • Season: Oct. 3-Nov. 14, 2026, with 30 scheduled games per team
  • Teams: six clubs, stocked with prospects from all 30 MLB organizations
  • Home Run Derby: Sat., Nov. 7, 6:30 p.m., Salt River Fields
  • Fall Stars Game: Sun., Nov. 8, 6 p.m., Salt River Fields, MLB Network
  • Postseason: quarterfinals Nov. 12; semifinals Nov. 13; championship Nov. 14, 6 p.m.
  • Tickets: $14 adults, $12 seniors 60+ and teens 11-17, free for 10 and under

The Scottsdale Teams

Scottsdale Scorpions (Scottsdale Stadium)

Prospects from the Astros, Brewers, Giants, Rays and Tigers. Scottsdale Stadium sits in Old Town, so a game pairs easily with dinner nearby.

Salt River Rafters (Salt River Fields at Talking Stick)

Prospects from the Diamondbacks, Nationals, Rockies, Twins and Yankees. Salt River Fields also hosts Fall Stars Weekend.

Remaining Games Near Scottsdale

Scottsdale Stadium (Scorpions Home)

  • Wed., Nov. 4: vs. Salt River, 6:30 p.m.
  • Fri., Nov. 6: vs. Glendale, 6:30 p.m.

Salt River Fields (Rafters Home and Special Events)

  • Sat., Nov. 7: Home Run Derby, 6:30 p.m.
  • Sun., Nov. 8: Fall Stars Game, 6 p.m.
  • Tue., Nov. 10: Rafters vs. Peoria, 6:30 p.m.

Scorpions on the Road

  • Thu., Nov. 5: at Surprise, 6:30 p.m.
  • Tue., Nov. 10: at Surprise, 1:30 p.m.
  • Wed., Nov. 11: at Glendale, 3:30 p.m.

Monday, Nov. 9, is an off day league-wide.

The Postseason

All six clubs make the postseason:

  • Thu., Nov. 12, Sloan Park (Mesa): quarterfinals, No. 3 vs. No. 6 and No. 4 vs. No. 5, at 1:30 and 6:30 p.m.
  • Fri., Nov. 13, Peoria Sports Complex: semifinals, with the top two seeds joining, at 1:30 and 6:30 p.m.
  • Sat., Nov. 14, Peoria Sports Complex: championship, 6 p.m., on MLB Network, MLB.com and the MLB App

Tickets and How to Watch

Single-Game Tickets

  • Adults: $14
  • Seniors 60+ and teens 11-17: $12
  • Kids 10 and under: free with an adult
  • Jewel Event games: $25 adults, $20 seniors and teens

Buy online through Brushfire or at the stadium box office on game day. Box offices are cashless.

Season Pass

General admission to every game at every stadium is $160, or $120 for seniors 60+, with a $31 Kids Club pass for 10 and under. It's a late-season buy now, but worth knowing for next fall.

Streaming

MLB says most games stream free on the Arizona Fall League website.
