Overview

Character You Can't Build New

Central Phoenix is anchored by 36 designated historic districts, each with its own architectural identity and preservation guidelines. The citywide median sale price sits near $437,000, up roughly 9.1% year-over-year, though sought-after pockets within the historic core routinely trade above that mark.

Buyers here aren't just purchasing square footage — they're buying into Spanish Colonial, Mission Revival, Tudor Revival, bungalow, and mid-century modern architecture on lots shaded by decades-old trees, a look and feel that simply isn't replicated in newer Valley suburbs.

Neighborhoods Worth Knowing

Willo

Between Thomas and McDowell Roads, Central and 7th Avenues — one of Phoenix's most walkable and photographed bungalow districts.

Encanto-Palmcroft

Bounded roughly by Encanto Blvd to McDowell Rd and 7th to 15th Avenues, prized for grand Tudor and Spanish Colonial homes facing Encanto Park.

Coronado

Roughly bounded by Virginia Avenue to Coronado Road, 8th to 14th Streets — an eclectic, up-and-coming mix of bungalows and mid-century homes.

North Central Corridor

Premium estate lots along Central Avenue, known for mid-century modern architecture and mature, canopy-shaded streets.

Roosevelt

Downtown, between Portland and Fillmore Streets and Central to 7th Avenues — home to the Roosevelt Row Arts District's galleries and monthly art walks.

North Encanto

Generally bounded by 19th Avenue on the west and 15th Avenue on the east, offering historic character at a more accessible price point.

Lifestyle & Landmarks

Culture at Every Corner

Encanto Park, one of the oldest municipal parks in the Southwest, anchors the Encanto-Palmcroft district with a lagoon, golf course, and shaded picnic grounds. The Heard Museum and Phoenix Art Museum sit just east of the historic core, while Roosevelt Row hosts a thriving gallery and mural scene downtown. For shopping and dining, the Biltmore corridor is a short drive from most Central Phoenix neighborhoods.

Common Questions

Central Phoenix Real Estate FAQ

What is the median home price in Central Phoenix?

As of 2026, the median home sale price across Central Phoenix is around $437,000, up about 9.1% year-over-year, though individual historic districts like Willo, Encanto-Palmcroft, and the North Central corridor often command a premium above that citywide figure.

What are the best historic districts in Central Phoenix?

Phoenix has 36 designated historic districts, with Central Phoenix favorites including Willo, Encanto-Palmcroft, Coronado, and the North Central corridor along Central Avenue. Architectural styles range from Spanish Colonial and Mission Revival to Tudor Revival, bungalows, and mid-century modern homes.

What is there to do in Central Phoenix?

Central Phoenix is home to Encanto Park, the Heard Museum and Phoenix Art Museum, the Roosevelt Row Arts District, and the shopping and dining of the Biltmore corridor, all within a short drive or bike ride of most historic neighborhoods.
