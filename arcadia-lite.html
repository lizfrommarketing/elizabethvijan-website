Overview

Arcadia's Character, A Different Price Point

Arcadia Lite refers to the neighborhoods bordering Arcadia proper, typically closer to 44th Street, where lots and homes run smaller than the estate-sized parcels found deeper into Arcadia. It shares the same mid-century ranch character, citrus-grove roots, and canal-side geography — making it a favorite for buyers who want the Arcadia lifestyle without the largest-lot price tag.

As of early 2026, the median home price sits near $1.35 million, with strong year-over-year appreciation and a sale-to-list ratio close to 100%, a sign that well-presented homes here are moving quickly and at or near asking price.

Buyer Notes

What to Know Before You Buy

Because Arcadia Lite sits at the edge of a high-heat corridor, buyers should ask about a home's orientation, shade coverage, and cooling efficiency — a conversation Elizabeth walks every client through given the neighborhood's projected increase in extreme-heat days over the coming decades.

Common Questions

Arcadia Lite Real Estate FAQ

What is the median home price in Arcadia Lite?

As of early 2026, the median home price in Arcadia Lite is approximately $1.35 million, with price per square foot around $513. Homes typically sell in about 44 days, close to full asking price.

What is the difference between Arcadia and Arcadia Lite?

Arcadia Lite refers to the more modestly sized lots and homes bordering Arcadia proper, generally closer to 44th Street. It shares Arcadia's mid-century ranch character and citrus-grove history but typically offers smaller lots and a somewhat lower price point, while still enjoying walkable access to Arcadia's dining scene.

Is Arcadia Lite a good investment?

Arcadia Lite has seen strong price appreciation, with year-over-year growth in the market approaching 90% in some reporting periods, reflecting continued demand for renovated ranch homes and proximity to Arcadia's restaurant corridor.
