Why Chaparral Park Is One of Scottsdale's Best-Kept Secrets

Ask most out-of-state buyers about Scottsdale and they'll picture North Scottsdale's golf estates. Ask a local, and Chaparral Park in the 85250 zip code is often the first neighborhood they mention — and for good reason.

A Park That Anchors the Neighborhood

Chaparral Park itself spans roughly 78 acres and includes a lake, walking paths, sports fields, a dog park, an aquatic center, and a disc golf course. It's one of the most amenity-rich public parks in the city, and it sits right in the middle of the neighborhood that shares its name.

Location Is the Whole Story

This pocket of the 85250 zip code sits between Old Town Scottsdale and the Camelback Corridor, putting residents within easy walking or biking distance of Scottsdale Fashion Square, Old Town's restaurant and gallery scene, and the resort corridor's spa and dining options — without the price tag or drive time of North Scottsdale's gated golf estates.

A Mix of Established and New

The neighborhood's housing stock reflects decades of steady, desirable demand: established mid-century homes on mature, tree-lined lots sit alongside newer custom builds, giving buyers a range of price points and architectural styles within the same walkable radius of the park.

Why It Moves Fast

Central location, genuine walkability, and a well-loved community park are a hard combination to find in Scottsdale at this price point, which is exactly why well-priced listings here tend to move quickly. Buyers who wait too long to act often find themselves competing for the next listing instead.

What zip code is Chaparral Park in?

Chaparral Park sits within Scottsdale's 85250 zip code, a centrally located area between Old Town Scottsdale and the Camelback Corridor.

What is there to do at Chaparral Park?

Chaparral Park spans roughly 78 acres and includes a lake, walking paths, sports fields, a dog park, an aquatic center, and a disc golf course, making it one of Scottsdale's most amenity-rich public parks.

Why do buyers like the Chaparral Park neighborhood?

Buyers are drawn to the neighborhood's central location, walkability to the park and Old Town Scottsdale and Fashion Square, mature tree-lined streets, and a mix of established mid-century homes and newer custom builds.
